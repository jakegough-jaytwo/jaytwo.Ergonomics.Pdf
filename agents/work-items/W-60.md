# W-60. Materialize the closed B items as done W records

## Problem

Under D-33 and D-36, the open B items became W records, but the 41 closed B items existed only in the backlog and report history: the collapsed "Closed" entries in the archive removed under D-36/W-56, earlier full entries from the legacy B-item checkpoints, and role reports removed under D-35/W-55. Those pre-removal tree states are [squashed]. Nothing in the tree records what those items scoped, what they changed, or how they closed, so `make agents-status` and `agents/work-items/` show only part of the project's history.

## Why now

The owner asked for closed W records built from the B history, as consistent as possible with the current work-item standard.

## Scope and exclusions

Create one `agents/work-items/W-NN.md` per closed B item, with the same number and the B ID in `legacy_ids`, as D-33 and D-36 already specify for a reopened item: B-01 to B-12, B-15 to B-17, B-19 to B-39, B-41, and B-43 to B-46. Each record uses the template's front matter and sections. Problem, scope, and acceptance come from the item's earliest full backlog entry, or from the report that filed it when the backlog only ever held the closed form. Closeout comes from the closed entry and the reports it cites. `allowed_changes` lists the paths the item's reports say it changed. Record the decision in D-37, and update the history note in `agents/README.md`.

Exclusions: no change to the six already-migrated items (W-13, W-14, W-18, W-40, W-42, W-47), to `agents/state/current.md`, to any runtime, test, or product document, or to existing reports. Nothing is reopened. Missing history is recorded as missing, not reconstructed.

## Acceptance criteria

- 41 new W records exist, one per closed B item, each `done` or `cancelled` with a completion date, all seven template sections, and non-empty `allowed_changes`.
- Every claim in a record traces to the backlog history or a report it names.
- `make agents-check`, `make agents-test`, and `make task-check` pass.

## Evidence required

Record-only change. Workflow checks.
