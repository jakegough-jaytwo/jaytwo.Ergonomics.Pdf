# W-115. Cut object parsing allocations

## Problem

One walk of the 500-page form decodes about 1.8 MB and allocates about 44 MiB, so parsing allocates roughly 24 bytes per decoded byte. Every form path pays it. Extraction allocates 68 MiB against iText's 12 MiB, and sign 105 MiB against 20 MiB (`agents/evidence/itext-comparison.md`).

## Why now

After W-114, this is the remaining cost on every form path.

## Scope and exclusions

- Profile allocations on form extraction and the field walk, and record the largest allocation sites in Notes.
- Reduce the largest sites, for example by interning names, using a compact representation for small dictionaries, and avoiding boxing and copies of decoded buffers. Measure each change.
- Excluded: object-stream retention (W-109), public API, and any change to what is parsed or accepted.

## Acceptance criteria

- Outputs are byte-identical on the G7 workloads, the interop tests, and the corpus round trips. Every parser hardening and fuzz regression test passes, and parse errors keep their offsets.
- Allocation per decoded byte on form extraction is at most half of today's, measured in `make compare-itext` and recorded.
- G8: every fuzz target reruns from the changed code at the campaign's predeclared allocation, as in W-108, recorded in `status.md`.

## Evidence required

Internally tested, plus the G8 rerun.
