# W-107. Record the owner's disposition of security findings SR-1 and SR-2

## Problem

W-18 closed with SR-1 (the host signer's output is not cryptographically checked) and SR-2 (an RFC 3161 token's signature is not cryptographically checked) as explicit G8 release blockers pending the owner. Each is Confirmed Critical under the security review's hostile-callback model, and each collides with the charter's exclusion of signature validation.

## Why now

The owner reviewed how other signing libraries and Acrobat behave and approved the current implementation on 2026-10-22. The G8 record still lists both findings as release blockers.

## Scope and exclusions

Record the owner's disposition and the ecosystem comparison on SR-1 in the security review report. Mark both findings accepted as documented limitations in the report and the G8 status row. State the limitation in `README.md`. No product code, test, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/spec/`, or `QUALIFICATION.md` change.

## Acceptance criteria

The security review report records the owner's disposition of SR-1 and SR-2 and the research behind it, with sources. The G8 status row no longer lists either finding as a release blocker. `README.md` says the library does not verify the host's signature value or a timestamp token's signature.

## Evidence required

Owner approval, given in the session on 2026-10-22. The research is web sources and a reading of EU DSS source on its master branch, recorded with links.

## Notes

- 2026-10-22: The owner asked whether the test harness checks validity, and what other libraries and Acrobat do. The tests check validity independently: pyHanko and Poppler `pdfsig` verify the library's signatures, and pyHanko and `openssl ts -verify` verify OpenSSL timestamp tokens. `SigningInteropTests.pdfsig_and_pyhanko_reject_a_tampered_byte_and_a_signature_from_another_key` shows that a wrong-key signer completes, and both tools reject the result. The gap is runtime only. The owner approved the current implementation and asked for the research highlights on SR-1.

## Closeout

- SR-1 and SR-2 are accepted by the owner as documented limitations, consistent with EU DSS, iText, and PDFBox, which do not verify an externally produced signature value when signing. The ecosystem comparison is recorded on SR-1 in `agents/reports/2026-10-22-W-18-security-review.md`. The G8 status row and `README.md` state the limitation.
- Verification: `make agents-check` and the staged `make task-check` passed. Documentation and records only; no build or test was needed.
- Limitations: the iText and PDFBox behavior is stated from general knowledge of their external-signing APIs, not from a source reading in this session. The DSS reading is of its master branch, not a tagged release. Completed 2026-10-22.
