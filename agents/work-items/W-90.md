# W-90. Leave usage rights in place on a signed document

## Problem

W-88 found that on a document certified at `P` 2 or `P` 3 and carrying `/Perms /UR3`, every write that removes `/UR3` makes pyHanko 0.38.0 report the certification broken (`modification_level=OTHER`, `docmdp_ok=False`, `ILLEGAL_MODIFICATIONS`), in 56 of 56 cases: document timestamps, signatures into a new field, and fills or signatures the transform does not grant. pyHanko also reports `OTHER` for earlier approval signatures when `/UR3` is removed, including the corpus file `cms-40b-pyhanko-approved`. When `/Perms` is left unchanged, pyHanko accepts the result (16 of 16).

## Why now

The owner chose option B from the W-88 proposal on 2026-10-20 and extended it to every document that already has a signature.

## Scope and exclusions

- On a document that already has a signature (approval, certification, or document timestamp), no write changes `/Perms`: `/UR3` stays even when its transform does not grant the write. Every other refusal, DocMDP and FieldMDP rule, and permission check is unchanged.
- On an unsigned document, the W-67 and W-87 policy is unchanged.
- Change `AGENTS.md` and spec §7.3 to the approved text below, record the decision as D-45, and update `ACCEPTANCE.md` and `README.md`.
- Excluded: validating the UR3 signature, the legacy `/Perms /UR` entry, and adding a way for callers to remove `/UR3`.

Approved text (owner, 2026-10-20).

`AGENTS.md`, replacing "Usage rights (`/UR3`) are kept only when they provably permit the write and are otherwise removed in the same revision (spec §7.3).":

> Usage rights (`/UR3`) are kept when they provably permit the write. Otherwise they are removed in the same revision, except on a document that already has a signature, where they are left in place (spec §7.3).

Spec §7.3 (`docs/spec/phase-1.md`), a new paragraph after the existing text:

> On a document that already has a signature (approval, certification, or document timestamp), a write never changes `/Perms`: `/UR3` stays even when its transform does not grant the write, because removing it is a change no signature permits, and validators report the earlier signatures as modified. Adobe Reader may then report that its extended features are unavailable.

## Acceptance criteria

Refine these when the item becomes active. At minimum: in `UsageRightsTests`, every write on a signed document (approval, certified `P` 2 and `P` 3, document timestamp) leaves `/Perms` byte-for-byte unchanged in the new revision, whatever the transform grants; the unsigned cases are unchanged. pyHanko difference analysis accepts the W-88 removal cases rebuilt as tests (certification `docmdp_ok=True`; earlier approval signatures not `OTHER` because of `/Perms`). A signing interop or corpus check covers `cms-40b-pyhanko-approved`.

Refined on activation (2026-10-20):

1. `UsageRightsTests`: for each of the 17 writes and 31 policy cases, on a document signed by an approval signature, a DocMDP `P` 2 or `P` 3 certification, or a document timestamp, the new revision rewrites none of the policy's `/Perms` and `/UR3` objects, the catalog's `/Perms` value is unchanged, and `/UR3` is present exactly when it was before. At `P` 2 and `P` 3, metadata and attachment writes are refused with `PdfCertificationLockedException` and write nothing. The unsigned matrix (17 by 31) is unchanged and runs on a base document whose third signature field is empty.
2. A fill and a metadata write in one save keep `/UR3` on a signed document and remove it on an unsigned one; refused writes still keep `/UR3` and leave the save byte-identical; a catalog `/Perms /DocMDP` alone counts as signed.
3. pyHanko 0.38.0: the W-88 built forms (UR3 transforms `fill`, `sign`, `noref`; indirect and direct `/Perms`), signed by pyHanko at `P` 2, at `P` 3, or with an approval signature, then given each W-88 write (fill; CMS sign into the existing field, into a new field, and after a fill; document timestamp into the existing and a new field). For every output: `qpdf --check` passes, `/UR3` stays, the earlier signature is intact and valid with modification `FORM_FILLING` or `LTA_UPDATES` and `docmdp_ok`, and the new signature is intact and valid over the entire file.
4. `cms-40b-pyhanko-approved`: after a fill, a metadata write, an attachment add, a CMS sign into `Signature-2` and into a new field, and document timestamps into `Signature-2` and a new field, `/UR3` stays, `Signature-1` is intact and valid, pyHanko never gives a `/Perms` change as the reason for a modification verdict, and it accepts (not `OTHER`, `docmdp_ok`) the metadata write and the sign and timestamp into a new field. `CorpusTests` expects `/UR3` kept after the metadata revision on that file, and its manifest note is updated.
5. The new tests fail on the pre-W-90 engine. `ACCEPTANCE.md` and `README.md` describe the behavior; `AGENTS.md`, spec §7.3, and `QUALIFICATION.md` carry the owner-approved text; D-45 records the decision.

## Evidence required

`UsageRightsTests`, pyHanko difference analysis on the W-88 cases, the corpus probe with qpdf, pypdf, and pyHanko, and the full suite on every target.
