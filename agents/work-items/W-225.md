# W-225. Record the owner-approved tag-structure exception and partial-selection attribute refusal in the adopted pages contract

## Problem

The adopted pages contract (W-218-PAGES-2026-10-31-R2) promises to preserve tags or refuse. It does not say what happens to retained tag structure when assembly omits a structure-owned annotation under the link and annotation rules, and it accepts pruned spanning elements without asking whether their attributes remain true after pruning. A read-only compatibility study found both cases in representative business PDFs: a handbook whose internal links are omitted by almost any partial selection, and tables whose cells lose neighbours when a page is dropped.

## Scope and exclusions

The owner approved two choices in the assigning conversation, D4-B and D5-A:

- D4-B: an omitted annotation's object reference is removed; a `Link` or `Annot` element left without its annotation becomes `Span`; an element left without content is removed; each adjustment is reported separately from the annotation omission; preservation of adjusted elements is not claimed. This is an owner-approved exception to tag preservation. A required form widget that cannot be retained still refuses.
- D5-A: a partial selection refuses before output when an affected attribute's truth after pruning cannot be established, with a closed safe set copied unchanged. No attribute is transformed.

Documentation only: `docs/spec/pages.md` (operative wording, preservation matrix, `PAGES-TAG-02`, `ASSEMBLY-TAG-01`, acceptance map, implementation gaps, adoption record) and the pages row of `docs/spec/README.md`. Out of scope: runtime code, `docs/behavior.md` (no verified change in current behavior), `docs/acceptance.md` (it lists only enforced outcomes, and these scenarios are unexecuted), `docs/supported.md`, the charter, and every other choice from the study: element identifier and IDTree rewriting, `Headers` rewriting beyond the loss refusal, `/E`, `/Info` and `/Metadata` handling, an untagged-derivative option, and the page-local subset of W-191.

## Acceptance criteria

- The preservation matrix, `PAGES-TAG-02`, `ASSEMBLY-TAG-01` and the acceptance map describe the same two rules.
- The exception is limited to structure whose annotation was omitted under the existing annotation or link rules; the contract states that preservation of the adjusted elements is not claimed.
- The attribute refusal happens before any destination write, and the safe set is closed.
- Two new acceptance scenarios are marked unexecuted; no result is claimed.
- Refusal timing, supported scope and every other preservation row are unchanged.
