# W-113. Plan closing the performance gap with iText

## Problem

W-111 measured this library 19x slower than iText on the 500-page metadata edit, with 85x the allocation, and about 5x the allocation on form extraction and sign (`agents/evidence/itext-comparison.md`).

## Why now

The owner asked on 2026-10-22 for the changes that close the gap, and for a brief on the certification-detection trade-off to take to a separate reviewer.

## Scope and exclusions

- `agents/reports/2026-10-22-performance-gap.md`: the causes, measured with a stage probe, and the ordered changes W-114, W-115, W-109, and W-116.
- `agents/reports/2026-10-22-certification-detection-brief.md`: a self-contained brief for an outside reviewer on whether writes may detect certification without walking the field tree.
- Excluded: any code change.

## Acceptance criteria

Both reports exist, and the items they name exist with scope and criteria.

## Evidence required

The stage probe and the corpus survey are recorded in the reports. They are measurements from this session, not gates.

## Notes

- 2026-10-22: The stage probe was a throwaway test in the comparison project, deleted after the run. The corpus `/SigFlags` and `/Perms` survey used pypdf on `test/jaytwo.Ergonomics.Pdf.Tests/Fixtures/corpus`. Two encrypted files did not open in that script and are not listed.

- Observed: Setting the title on the 500-page form costs 43 ms and 45 MiB, the same as reading every field, and the save repeats it. `CertificationLevel()` walks the whole field tree, page index included, on every write.
- Limitations: The effect estimates in the report are estimates. W-114 and W-115 measure them. Completed 2026-10-22.
