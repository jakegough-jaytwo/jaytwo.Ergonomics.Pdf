# W-156. Design a bounded PDF admission inspection API

## Problem

Enterprise hosts need a bounded, read-only way to identify known active-content mechanisms and reject documents by an explicit policy. Successful parsing or an incremental save must not be confused with admission, sanitization, or physical removal of prior content.

## Scope and exclusions

Design, but do not implement, structured inspection findings, explicit completeness, and policy evaluation for JavaScript, launch actions, external references, form actions, embedded files, and other selected interactive structures. Address unknown, unsupported, encrypted, unreachable, and uninspected structures. Full reconstruction, CDR, sanitization, rendering, and malware scanning remain outside scope.

## Acceptance criteria

- The proposed API distinguishes parse success, inspection completeness, policy verdict, and sanitization.
- An affirmative admission verdict is impossible when policy-relevant content is unknown, unsupported, encrypted, or uninspected.
- The proposal defines bounded traversal, work limits, cancellation, structured locations and reasons, and extensibility without exposing the internal object model.
- The owner can approve, reject, or revise the contract; approval creates implementation work items and any needed spec change.

## Evidence required

An API proposal exercised against representative documents for each selected mechanism, including nested or indirect actions and at least one incomplete-inspection case.
