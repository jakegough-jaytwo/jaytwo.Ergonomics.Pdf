# W-140. Keep the W-99 unsigned-/P fixtures out of the G2 corpus

## Problem

1. **Summary.** W-99 added `pypdf-unsigned-p-r2.pdf` through `pypdf-unsigned-p-r6.pdf` to `test/jaytwo.Pdf.Tests/Fixtures` without corpus manifest entries.
2. **Impact.** `CorpusManifestTests.every_fixture_pdf_has_a_manifest_entry` fails on every framework, so the R1 checkpoint gate (`make docker-builder docker-test`, W-134) fails. The test was in `Lane.Corpus`, which the fast lane W-99 ran leaves out.
3. **Reproduction.** `make quiet-docker-test` at e65e9e5.
4. **Expected.** Every committed fixture PDF is accounted for.
5. **Actual.** `No manifest entry: pypdf-unsigned-p-r2.pdf, pypdf-unsigned-p-r4.pdf, pypdf-unsigned-p-r5.pdf, pypdf-unsigned-p-r3.pdf, pypdf-unsigned-p-r6.pdf`.
6. **Severity.** Medium: a release gate fails; no product behavior is wrong.

Adding them as corpus entries was tried and measured: the corpus would fall to 29 externally authored files of 46, below the G2 two-thirds rule, and pyHanko 0.38.0 cannot open the revision 5 file, which fails three corpus interop suites. The owner chose D-59 instead.

## Scope and exclusions

Under D-59: move the five fixtures to `Fixtures/defects/`, record them in `Fixtures/defects/fixtures.json`, make `CorpusManifestTests` enforce the fixture directories in the fast lane, and add `DefectFixtureTests` for the register. No library change, no corpus manifest change, and no change to the W-99 assertions.

## Acceptance criteria

- `CorpusManifestTests` and `DefectFixtureTests` pass, and both run in the fast lane.
- The W-99 tests (`ForeignPdfTests.pypdf_unsigned_permissions_file_is_filled_signed_and_accepted`, `WriterQuirkTests.pypdf_unsigned_permissions_read_as_qpdf_reads_them`) pass from the new location.
- A fixture PDF added to `Fixtures/defects/` without a register entry, or anywhere else under `Fixtures/` without a manifest entry, fails a fast-lane test.

## Evidence required

Focused runs of those classes on net10.0, the fast-lane run of the two register tests, and a net48 build. The W-134 Docker gate reruns them on every framework.

## Closeout

- The five fixtures are in `Fixtures/defects/`, recorded in `Fixtures/defects/fixtures.json` with pyHanko 0.38.0 `unsupported` on revision 5 (D-59). The G2 corpus is unchanged at 41 entries.
- `CorpusManifestTests.every_fixture_pdf_has_a_manifest_entry` now scans `Fixtures/` recursively, fails a PDF outside the three fixture directories and a defect fixture listed as a corpus entry, and runs in the fast lane. `DefectFixtureTests` checks registration, hashes, named tests (by reflection), and validator results.
- `make quiet-test TEST_FRAMEWORK=net10.0 TEST_FILTER="FullyQualifiedName~CorpusManifestTests|FullyQualifiedName~DefectFixtureTests|FullyQualifiedName~pypdf_unsigned_permissions"`: 28/28 passed. The same filter passed 28/28 on net48 and in the netstandard2.0 lane.
- `make quiet-test LANE=fast TEST_FRAMEWORK=net10.0` on the two register tests and the fixture test: with a stray `defects/stray.pdf` and `other/stray.pdf` in the output directory both tests failed, naming the file; without them, 3/3 passed.
- `make quiet-build`: pass.
- Limitation: the register's `pass` results restate what the named tests assert; the tests, not the register, establish them on each run.
