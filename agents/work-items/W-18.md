# W-18. Complete fuzz qualification and independent security review

## Problem

Gate G8 still lacks a completed bounded fuzz record and an independent review of the parser and signer. The harness and fixed-seed smoke suite now exist, but the campaign artifacts have not been fully deduplicated, minimized, or triaged.

## Why now

G8 is release-blocking.

## Scope and exclusions

Complete the campaign within D-23's 24 aggregate CPU-hour cap, triage its artifacts, turn confirmed defects into regressions, rerun affected targets, and record the result. Obtain a hostile-input review in a fresh context that did not implement the code. Do not log secrets or add runtime dependencies.

## Acceptance criteria

The fuzz record names the seed, entry points, duration, target outcomes, triage dispositions, and rerun result. Critical and High findings are fixed or remain explicit release blockers. The independent review records its reviewer context, scope, and findings, as required by `QUALIFICATION.md` section 10.

## Evidence required

Internally tested fuzz regressions plus a genuinely independent security review.
