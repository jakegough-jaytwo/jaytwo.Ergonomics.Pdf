# W-77. Flatten form fields

## Problem

Forms cannot be flattened (spec W3, §7.1, §7.2).

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 19. It is in the owner's original charter.

## Scope and exclusions

- Flatten all fields or named fields as §7.1 describes, without decoding existing content streams.
- The signed-document opt-in of §7.2.
- Excluded: flattening other annotation types.

## Acceptance criteria

- Flattened output renders identically to the filled form in Poppler and MuPDF, including rotated pages and nonzero origins.
- Signed signature fields are never flattened.
- An opted-in flatten after an approval signature leaves that signature's bytes intact while a verifier reports the modification.

Refine these criteria, and the `ACCEPTANCE.md` rows they add or change, when the item becomes active. A write item also meets every check in plan C4.

## Evidence required

Two renderers; two verifiers.
