# W-61. Write the Phase 1 and Phase 2 specifications

## Problem

The owner's charter includes flattening, page extraction, and concatenation. The repository charter in `README.md` and `AGENTS.md` excluded extraction and concatenation from the first commit and never mentioned flattening. There was no written specification for the product the owner wants.

## Why now

Release qualification for the narrower scope (W-13, W-14, W-18) was next in the queue. Qualifying a scope that is about to change wastes that work.

## Scope and exclusions

Write `docs/spec/phase-1.md` (adopted) and `docs/spec/phase-2.md` (draft outline with interview questions), and record the owner's decisions as D-38. Record the owner's approval of the async compatibility package as D-39. Write `agents/reference/charter-reset-plan.md`, the plan for the charter update, workflow slimming, and re-planning, for independent review before any of it starts.

Excluded: changes to `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, `ACCEPTANCE.md`, `QUALIFICATION.md`, skills, source, and tests. Those follow in separate items with owner approval.

## Acceptance criteria

- `docs/spec/phase-1.md` states every Phase 1 decision from the 2026-10-19 interview, marks each requirement as existing, changed, or new, and lists the open questions the owner flagged.
- `docs/spec/phase-2.md` states the adopted Phase 2 scope and the questions for its interview.
- D-38 records the decision and names the documents it conflicts with.
- `make agents-check` and `make task-check` pass.

## Evidence required

Owner review of the two specifications. Documentation only; no build or test evidence.
