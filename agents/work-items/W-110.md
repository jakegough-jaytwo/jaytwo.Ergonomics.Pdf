# W-110. Let decision records commit as planning records

## Problem

`task-check` exempted work-item records and `agents/state/current.md` from the owner check, but not decision records. D-49, W-108, and W-109 could not be committed: with D-49 staged the commit needed an owning item, and without it W-108 and W-109 cited an unknown decision. The agent spent several turns probing the check and proposed a placeholder item to route around it.

## Why now

Five planning files were left uncommitted ahead of W-80. The owner asked for the process to be fixed so this does not recur.

## Scope and exclusions

Treat `agents/decisions/*.md` as planning records in `is_record_path`. Add tests. Record D-50. Update the exemption sentence in `AGENTS.md` and add the planning-record rule to `pdf-task-workflow`, with the owner's approved wording. Committing D-49, W-108, W-109, and the queue change is a separate record-only commit, not part of this item.

## Acceptance criteria

A staged transaction of a new decision, a work item citing it, and `agents/state/current.md` passes `task-check` with no active item. Work paths without an owner still fail. The tooling tests pass.

## Evidence required

`make agents-test`, `make agents-check`, and the staged `make task-check`.
