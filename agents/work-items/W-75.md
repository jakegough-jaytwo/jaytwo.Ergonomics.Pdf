# W-75. Attachment relationship, dates, checksum, compression, and page mode

## Problem

Attachments are always `Unspecified`, carry no dates or checksum, are stored uncompressed, and cannot open the attachments panel (spec R8, W9 to W13).

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 16.

## Scope and exclusions

- Spec R8 and W9 to W13, including the PDF/A-3 relationship subset and the conformance non-claim in W9.
- Excluded: page-level attachment annotations, and PDF/A, Factur-X, or ZUGFeRD conformance.

## Acceptance criteria

- An independent reader sees the relationship, dates, and checksum.
- Compressed content round-trips byte for byte.
- A PDF/A-3 input stays at effective version 1.7.

Refine these criteria, and the `ACCEPTANCE.md` rows they add or change, when the item becomes active. A write item also meets every check in plan C4.

## Evidence required

Independent reader; veraPDF credited only per property if used.
