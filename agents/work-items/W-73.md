# W-73. List revisions

## Problem

There is no way to list a file's revisions or tell whether one came after the last signature (spec R10).

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 14.

## Scope and exclusions

- Revisions with end offsets and signature coverage, reading only cross-reference sections and trailers.
- A linearized file's first-page and main sections count as one revision.

## Acceptance criteria

- Revision counts and offsets agree with an independent reader on the corpus, including linearized files.

Refine these criteria, and the `ACCEPTANCE.md` rows they add or change, when the item becomes active. A write item also meets every check in plan C4.

## Evidence required

Independent reader on the corpus.
