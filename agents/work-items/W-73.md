# W-73. List revisions

## Problem

There is no way to list a file's revisions or tell whether one came after the last signature (spec R10).

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 14.

## Scope and exclusions

- `PdfDocument.Revisions`, in oldest-to-newest order, with a 1-based number, end offset, and whether a signature field's `/ByteRange` claims to cover exactly that revision. Coverage is reported, not validated.
- Revision boundaries come only from cross-reference sections, trailers, and their `startxref` / `%%EOF` footers; signed byte ranges are never read. Signature coverage reads the field tree and signature dictionaries, but not CMS `/Contents`.
- A linearized file's first-page and main sections count as one revision. A hybrid table and its `/XRefStm` also count as one revision.
- `PdfSignatureInfo` reports the logical revision number matching its signed end offset, or null when the byte range does not end at a listed revision.
- Excluded: cryptographic validation, trust, modification-policy analysis, and repair scanning for missing cross-reference data or revision footers.

## Acceptance criteria

Refined at activation on 2026-10-21. `ACCEPTANCE.md` gains a Revisions section.

- `PdfDocument.Revisions` lists logical revisions oldest first. Each `PdfRevision` has a 1-based `Number`, its exclusive `EndOffset`, and `IsCoveredBySignature`, which is true when a terminal signature field has a structurally valid `/ByteRange` ending at that offset. `PdfSignatureInfo.SignedRevisionNumber` names the same logical revision; a byte range ending elsewhere leaves it null. Nothing is validated and no signed bytes are read.
- Counts, end offsets, signature coverage, and signed-revision numbers agree with pyHanko 0.38.0 on every corpus file that opens, the pyHanko signed fixture, this library's signature followed by an unsigned revision, and classic, xref-stream, and hybrid inputs.
- A linearized file's first-page and main xref sections are one logical revision, including the linearized IRS W-9 with a later incremental section. A hybrid table and its `/XRefStm` are also one revision.
- Listing revisions on a sparse signed file larger than 1 GiB reads less than 2 MiB and never the unread middle. Revision footer parsing is bounded and a missing `startxref` / `%%EOF` footer throws `PdfParseException` from `Revisions` without making other reads unavailable.

## Evidence required

Independent reader on the corpus.
