# W-71. PAdES baseline B-B signatures

## Problem

Signatures use `adbe.pkcs7.detached`, which EU recipients may not accept as PAdES (spec W17). W-51 found that a size overflow reports a lower bound instead of the exact size; that is folded in here.

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 12.

## Scope and exclusions

- `ETSI.CAdES.detached` with `signing-certificate-v2`, no CMS `signing-time`, and the time in `/M`.
- With a timestamp authority, PAdES B-T.
- Report the exact overflow size (former W-51).
- Excluded: B-LT and B-LTA.

## Acceptance criteria

- B-B and B-T output validates against the profile with an independent validator.
- `PdfSignatureTooLargeException` reports the exact required size.

Refine these criteria, and the `ACCEPTANCE.md` rows they add or change, when the item becomes active. A write item also meets every check in plan C4.

## Evidence required

pyHanko and `pdfsig`; profile validation for B-T.
