# W-87. Keep usage rights when signing a field the UR3 transform grants

## Problem

W-67 removes `/UR3` on every `Sign`, because spec §7.3 lists no signing right. ISO 32000-1 Table 255 defines `/Signature` `Modify` as permission to apply a digital signature to an existing signature form field, and Adobe grants it so that free Reader users can sign a Reader-extended form. Removing `/UR3` in that case drops extended features that the transform explicitly allows.

## Why now

The owner decided on 2026-10-20, reviewing W-67, that signing should keep `/UR3` when the transform grants it, and approved the §7.3 wording below.

## Scope and exclusions

- `Sign` into an existing empty signature field (`FieldName` names it) keeps `/UR3` when the UR3 transform's `/Signature` array contains `Modify`. The other W-67 conditions still apply: an absent, unresolved, malformed, or unknown transform removes `/UR3`.
- `Sign` that creates a new signature field, and a document timestamp, still remove `/UR3`.
- Change §7.3 of `docs/spec/phase-1.md` to the approved text, record the decision as D-44 (it also confirms that replacing an attachment of the same name is `/EF` `Modify`), and update `ACCEPTANCE.md` and `README.md`.
- Excluded: validating the UR3 signature; the legacy `/Perms /UR` entry; certified and Reader-extended files beyond what W-88 settles.

Approved §7.3 text (owner, 2026-10-20):

> When the catalog `/Perms` has a `/UR3` signature, a write keeps it when its transform parameters grant that operation:
>
> - `/Form` `FillIn` for fills;
> - `/EF` `Create`, `Delete`, or `Modify` for attachment edits; replacing an attachment that has the same name is `Modify`;
> - `/Signature` `Modify` for `Sign` into an existing empty signature field (ISO 32000-1 Table 255).
>
> Any other write removes `/UR3` from `/Perms` in the same revision, so Adobe Reader drops the extended features silently instead of reporting a broken document. A `Sign` that creates a new signature field and a document timestamp are such writes.

## Acceptance criteria

Refine these when the item becomes active. At minimum, the `UsageRightsTests` sign cases split into existing-field and new-field cases. An existing field with `/Signature` `Modify` keeps `/UR3`, and every other sign case removes it. A failed sign still leaves `/UR3` as it was. The real Reader-extended corpus forms that have an empty signature field and grant `/Signature` `Modify` keep `/UR3` after signing, and the new and earlier signatures stay valid under pyHanko.

## Evidence required

`UsageRightsTests`, the corpus probe with qpdf, pypdf, and pyHanko, and the full suite on every target.
