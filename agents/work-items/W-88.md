# W-88. Check signing a certified Reader-extended file

## Problem

On a file that is both certified at DocMDP `P` 2 or `P` 3 and Reader-extended, a `Sign` that removes `/UR3` changes the catalog `/Perms` in the signed revision. `P` 2 and `P` 3 allow form fill and signing, and a validator that compares revisions may judge a `/Perms` change as outside them and report the certification as broken. After W-87, this still happens whenever the UR3 transform does not grant the sign. No corpus file is both certified and Reader-extended, so the behavior is untested.

## Why now

The owner asked on 2026-10-20, reviewing W-67, for a follow-up to test the case rather than change behavior without evidence.

## Scope and exclusions

- Build a fixture that is certified at `P` 2 and carries a `/UR3` signature, or find a real one, and sign it in the cases where `/UR3` is removed.
- Check the output with pyHanko's difference analysis of certified documents, and record whether it accepts the revision.
- If it reports the certification as broken, propose the fix to the owner: refuse `Sign` on such files, or leave `/UR3` in place for them. Do not choose between them in this item.
- Excluded: validating the UR3 signature, and changing behavior before the owner decides.

## Acceptance criteria

The fixture and pyHanko's verdict for each removal case are recorded in Notes, and either no change is needed or a proposal is in front of the owner.

## Evidence required

pyHanko's validation of the signed output.
