# W-72. Inspect existing signatures

## Problem

There is no way to list existing signatures, their signers, times, coverage, or certification level (spec R9).

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 13. It parses untrusted CMS, so it lands before the G8 campaign.

## Scope and exclusions

- The fields R9 lists, for every signature field, without reading the signed byte range.
- Excluded: trust, revocation, and cryptographic validation.

## Acceptance criteria

- Inspection agrees with pyHanko and `pdfsig` on the corpus and on this library's own RSA, ECDSA, and PAdES output.
- Inspecting a 1 GiB signed file reads no signed range.

Refine these criteria, and the `ACCEPTANCE.md` rows they add or change, when the item becomes active. A write item also meets every check in plan C4.

## Evidence required

Agreement with two verifiers.
