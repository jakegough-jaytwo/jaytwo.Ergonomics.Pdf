# W-209. Measure decoder use at required decoding sites

## Problem

Raw byte searches cannot establish decoder compatibility inside compressed objects, and copied streams differ from consumed streams.

Source: [simplification charter](../reference/reset-siimplify.md), PLAN 18 / O 10. Ticketing baseline: `82a83855581115400d4ecb5f6884895c20ecb45b`; reverify observations at execution. This records a research question, not a verified defect.

## Scope and exclusions

Inventory ASCIIHex/ASCII85/LZW/RunLength, abbreviations, chains, predictors and each required decoding site across xref/object streams, metadata and attachments. Compare retention with precisely bounded removal candidates.

Research and draft proposals only. Exclude runtime/test-suite changes, protected-file edits, shipped-contract changes, active-queue changes and implementation. Store findings, exact wording proposals and bounded disposable probe material under `agents/reference/simplification/W-209/`. ## Acceptance criteria

- Resolve and inspect required streams in the pinned corpus; do not count encoded page/image copying as decoder demand.
- Provide minimal required-stream fixtures per candidate filter, including malformed data and decompression limits.
- Quantify compatibility loss, unsupported-filter error, caller recovery outside this library and real implementation responsibility saved.
- Recommend retain/change with exact proposed clauses and effort ranges; missing use in this corpus is not proof of no business need.

## Evidence required
