# W-78. Rotate pages

## Problem

Pages cannot be rotated (spec W6, §7.2).

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 20.

## Scope and exclusions

- Set `/Rotate` on the page object, normalized to 0, 90, 180, or 270.
- The signed-document opt-in of §7.2.

## Acceptance criteria

- An independent reader and renderer see the rotation, including over an inherited `/Rotate`.
- Field and box coordinates are unchanged.

Refine these criteria, and the `ACCEPTANCE.md` rows they add or change, when the item becomes active. A write item also meets every check in plan C4.

## Evidence required

Independent reader and renderer.
