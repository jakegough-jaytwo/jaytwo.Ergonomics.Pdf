# W-198. Specify explicit hybrid-XFA preparation

## Problem

Implicit hybrid conversion can abandon valuable XFA behavior without a deliberate caller decision.

Source: [simplification charter](../reference/reset-siimplify.md), PLAN 12 / O 07. Ticketing baseline: `82a83855581115400d4ecb5f6884895c20ecb45b`; reverify observations at execution. This records a research question, not a verified defect.

## Scope and exclusions

Research a named preparation step retaining supported AcroForm representation while deliberately abandoning XFA. Compare integrated Combine preparation with a documented separate step; dynamic/XFA-only editing remains excluded.

Research and draft proposals only. Exclude runtime/test-suite changes, protected-file edits, shipped-contract changes, active-queue changes and implementation. Store findings, exact wording proposals and bounded disposable probe material under `agents/reference/simplification/W-198/`. ## Acceptance criteria

- Define permissions, signed/certified refusal, ordering with rights removal, rollback, repeated invocation and typed failures.
- Probe whether assembly observes pending edits before proposing one-session preparation.
- Measure caller steps, credentials, lost behavior and accepted-after-preparation cases with W-189; avoid double-counting Reader-rights savings.
- Deliver exact wording and a separate implementation scope; fill, flatten and assembly do not implicitly convert under the proposal.

Record measured versus inferred claims and missing tools/coverage explicitly. Run `make agents-check` and staged `make task-check` for planning records. No full framework matrix, fuzz campaign or Docker gate is required for this research item.
