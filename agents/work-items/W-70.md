# W-70. SHA-384, SHA-512, and ECDSA signing

## Problem

Signing supports RSA with SHA-256 only. Many tokens and HSMs are ECDSA-only (spec W15, W16).

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 11.

## Scope and exclusions

- ECDSA P-256 and P-384, with the host returning P1363 `r||s` and the library DER-encoding it.
- SHA-256, SHA-384, and SHA-512 for RSA and ECDSA.
- Excluded: other curves and RSA-PSS.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every key and digest combination verifies with pyHanko and `pdfsig`, including through SoftHSM.

Refine these criteria, and the `ACCEPTANCE.md` rows they add or change, when the item becomes active. A write item also meets every check in plan C4.

## Evidence required

Two PDF-aware verifiers.
