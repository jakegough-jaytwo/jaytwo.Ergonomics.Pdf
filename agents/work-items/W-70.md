# W-70. SHA-384, SHA-512, and ECDSA signing

## Problem

Signing supports RSA with SHA-256 only. Many tokens and HSMs are ECDSA-only (spec W15, W16).

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 11.

## Scope and exclusions

- `PdfDigestAlgorithm` (`Sha256`, the default, `Sha384`, `Sha512`) on `PdfSignatureOptions.DigestAlgorithm`, carried to providers on `PdfSignatureContext.DigestAlgorithm`.
- `IPdfExternalSigner.SignHashAsync` receives the raw digest and its `HashAlgorithmName` (spec W16). This is the pre-1.0 signer-contract change spec W15 and W16 require.
- The key type comes from the leaf certificate's `SubjectPublicKeyInfo`: `rsaEncryption` signs RSA PKCS#1 v1.5; `id-ecPublicKey` on P-256 or P-384 signs ECDSA, and the host returns the IEEE P1363 `r‖s` value (64 or 96 bytes), which the library DER-encodes (spec W15). Any other key is refused before the signer is called.
- The CMS digest and signature algorithm identifiers follow the chosen digest and key: `sha256`, `sha384`, `sha512`; `sha*WithRSAEncryption`; `ecdsa-with-SHA*`.
- Excluded: other curves, RSA-PSS, PAdES (W-71's spec W17), and changing the RFC 3161 imprint, which stays SHA-256 over the signature value. A document timestamp keeps SHA-256; asking for another digest with `PdfSignatureKind.DocTimeStamp` throws `PdfSignException` and writes nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

- RSA 2048, ECDSA P-256, and ECDSA P-384, each with SHA-256, SHA-384, and SHA-512, sign through in-memory .NET keys: `SignedCms.CheckSignature(true)` accepts each, the SignedData and SignerInfo digest algorithm and the SignerInfo signature algorithm carry the expected OIDs, and the `messageDigest` is the chosen digest of the byte ranges. pyHanko reports each intact, valid, and trusted with the expected digest and mechanism, and `pdfsig` reports each valid and trusted with the expected signing hash algorithm (new `ACCEPTANCE.md` signing row; rows 79, 83, and 108 restated for the chosen digest).
- The same nine combinations sign through SoftHSM 2 keys (`CKM_RSA_PKCS` with a host-built DigestInfo, `CKM_ECDSA` on the raw digest), and pyHanko and `pdfsig` trust each chain from the root.
- A leaf whose key is not RSA, P-256, or P-384 (P-521 in the test), an ECDSA signature value of the wrong length (a DER-encoded one in the test), an undefined `PdfDigestAlgorithm`, and a document timestamp with a digest other than SHA-256 each throw `PdfSignException` and do not extend the file; the leaf and digest cases leave `SignHashAsync` uncalled (row 84).
- `README.md` describes the signer contract with the algorithm argument, the P1363 form, and both key types, and moves ECDSA, SHA-384, and SHA-512 from target to supported. Spec W15 and W16 become Exists.

A write item also meets every check in plan C4.

## Evidence required

Two PDF-aware verifiers: pyHanko and Poppler `pdfsig`, for every key and digest combination, in memory and through SoftHSM.
