# W-46. Remove stale list-box /I on fill (F-4)

## Problem

Skill-review finding F-4: choice filling did not distinguish combo boxes from list boxes and did not update or remove `/I`. A list box that started with `/V (A)` and `/I [0]` and was filled with `B` wrote `/V (B)` but kept `/I [0]`.

## Why now

The skill plan (D-27, step 2) asked for a reproduction of the logical and rendered behavior, separating the appearance question from the stale index.

## Scope and exclusions

Fix the stale `/I` state. Keep the existing list-box appearance subset; widening it is an owner decision and out of scope.

## Acceptance criteria

A list-box fill leaves no stale `/I` beside the new `/V`. The regression test fails on the unfixed source. `README.md` and `ACCEPTANCE.md` state both the fix and the appearance limitation.

## Evidence required

Not stated in the original entry. Internally tested.
