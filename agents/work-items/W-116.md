# W-116. Decide whether writes may detect certification without walking the field tree

## Problem

Even after W-114, the first write on a document walks the whole field tree to find a certification, where iText does no walk. Skipping it would mean deciding certification from the catalog `/Perms /DocMDP`, and possibly from AcroForm `/SigFlags`, instead of from each signature field. That changes a documented certification rule.

## Why now

The owner asked for a brief to take to a separate reviewer: `agents/reports/2026-10-22-certification-detection-brief.md`.

## Scope and exclusions

- The owner chooses among the brief's options A to D, informed by the review. A decision record captures the choice. If the rule changes, an implementation item follows.
- Excluded: implementation.

## Acceptance criteria

A decision record, or a recorded decision to keep the walk.

## Evidence required

The outside review, plus any corpus evidence the decision asks for.
