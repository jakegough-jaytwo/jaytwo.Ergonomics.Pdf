# W-197. Define assembly refusal and explicit unsigned derivatives

## Problem

Meaningful omission cannot become successful preservation, and derivative consent cannot bypass authentication or document locks.

Source: [simplification charter](../reference/reset-siimplify.md), PLAN 04 / PLAN 06 / O 05. Ticketing baseline: `82a83855581115400d4ecb5f6884895c20ecb45b`; reverify observations at execution. This records a research question, not a verified defect.

## Scope and exclusions

Propose bounded whole-input versus selected-page admission for active behavior, attachments/AF, layers, unsupported appearances/relationships, signed inputs and XFA; tags depend on W-191. Distinguish approval signatures, certification, timestamps, rights, signed widgets, locks and painted marks. Coordinate W-156 without introducing its generic policy API through this effort.

Research and draft proposals only. Exclude runtime/test-suite changes, protected-file edits, shipped-contract changes, active-queue changes and implementation. Store findings, exact wording proposals and bounded disposable probe material under `agents/reference/simplification/W-197/`. Follow-up implementation gets its own owner-approved item and allowed paths.

## Acceptance criteria

- Enumerate inspected structures, retained graphs, budgets and incompleteness; never promise arbitrary JavaScript detection.
- Recommend a narrow default refusal contract and separately named explicit derivative only where justified, with valuable losses and remaining exclusions.
- Retain per-input permissions and AllowUnencryptedOutput; consent does not override DocMDP/FieldMDP. Content-painted marks remain outside removal scope.
- Specify preflight errors naming operation, input occurrence, structure/category and supported next step without sensitive details. Separate pre-output guarantees from source mutation, late reads and physical destination failure.
- Propose an explicit retain/rescope/supersede disposition for W-156 for owner approval; no silent cancellation or general StripUnsupported/BestEffort option.
