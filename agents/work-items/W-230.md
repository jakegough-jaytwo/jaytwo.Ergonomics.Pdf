# W-230. Narrow the pages contract's tag preservation to complete documents and contiguous ranges

## Problem

The adopted pages contract promises tag preservation for any selection of a tagged input, including repeated and reordered pages. W-225 left open how element identifiers stay unique across repeated copies, and the unresolved cases (repeated and interleaved tagged pages, repair of malformed structure) would make the first tag implementation much larger than the business cases it serves.

## Scope and exclusions

The owner approved a limited accessibility scope in conversation: tag preservation for ordinary full-document concatenation and contiguous-page extraction of well-formed tagged PDFs; repeated or interleaved tagged-page assembly, repair and accessibility-conformance claims are deferred. Documentation only: the tag clauses, matrix row, selection cross-references, acceptance targets, implementation gaps and adoption record in `docs/spec/pages.md`, and the pages row of `docs/spec/README.md`.

Out of scope: runtime code, `docs/behavior.md` and `docs/acceptance.md` (no verified behavior changes), `docs/supported.md`, the charter, selection rules for untagged inputs, and the Alt/ActualText/E handling of reduced elements, which stays undecided.

## Acceptance criteria

- The contract names the tagged-input subset, the outcome for mixed and for repeated or interleaved selections, the promised relationships and the refusal rule, with no PDF/UA claim.
- Untagged assembly selections are unchanged.
- Every clause whose only purpose was repeated or interleaved tagged-page support is removed or marked superseded; the W-225 exception and attribute rule are kept only where contiguous ranges still need them.
- New and changed acceptance targets are marked unexecuted.

## Evidence required

`make agents-check`; Markdown link and anchor check over the edited documents; `git diff --check`; a read of the diff against the criteria; `make task-check` at commit.
