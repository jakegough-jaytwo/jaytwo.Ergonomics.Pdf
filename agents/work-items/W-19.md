# W-19. Signer and timestamp failures do not leave a signature

## Problem

No test used a throwing `IPdfExternalSigner`, or showed that a timestamp failure is not reported as a successful timestamped signature.

## Why now

W-06 listed it as a gap for gates G4, G5, and G9.

## Scope and exclusions

`SignatureTests.cs` only. Add a throwing external signer and a throwing timestamp authority. This is not the W-04 imprint, digest-algorithm, or nonce check.

## Acceptance criteria

A throwing external signer leaves the destination bytes unchanged. A throwing timestamp authority throws `PdfTimestampException`, leaves the bytes unchanged, and does not leave a signature field.

## Evidence required

Internally tested.
