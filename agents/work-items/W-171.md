# W-171. Adopt the R9 and R10 inspection wording

## Problem

`docs/spec/phase-1.md` R9 says "whether it is signed" and "which revision it signs", and R10 says "whether a signature covers it". That language implies more than the structural inspection W-150 and W-170 ship.

## Scope and exclusions

Replace only those phrases with the wording from the owner's review of W-150 (W-170 Notes, question 15, approved by the owner on 2026-10-24). Every other R9 and R10 requirement, and both statuses, stay unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

R9 and R10 use the approved wording, and no requirement is added, removed, or moved.

## Evidence required

The diff of `docs/spec/phase-1.md`. The change is text only.

## Closeout

2026-10-24. Updated two rows of `docs/spec/phase-1.md`, both still Exists:

- **R9** now reports:
  - whether the field's `/V` resolves to a dictionary;
  - the `/ByteRange`'s structural status, its claimed ending offset, and the revision whose ending offset matches that claim, if any.

  It also states that none of these establishes cryptographic validity or trust.
- **R10** now reports:
  - each revision's structurally established end offset;
  - whether any signature `/ByteRange` endpoint coincides with it.

  It also states that offset coincidence is independent of validity and is not cryptographic coverage.

No other requirement text changed.
