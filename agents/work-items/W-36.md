# W-36. Parser and decoder hardening (audit SA-01, SA-02, SA-03, SA-04, SA-09, SA-11)

## Problem

From the D-23 security audit (`agents/reports/2026-10-18-security-auditor-B-18.md`), with the disposition in `2026-10-18-tech-lead-B-18-audit-review.md`:

- SA-01 (High): the literal-string escape and nesting branches, numbers, keywords, names, and hex skip the token cap.
- SA-02 (High): `ParseDictionary` work is unbounded and quadratic.
- SA-03 (High): xref-stream entry and object-stream header amplification.
- SA-04 (High): `WalkPages` has no depth bound, so the stack overflows.
- SA-09 (Medium): revision 4 substitutes `StdCF` for an omitted `StrF` or `StmF` instead of `Identity`.
- SA-11 (Medium): `FiltersOf` and `ParmsOf` misalign on malformed filter arrays.

## Why now

SA-01 to SA-04 are release blockers, and `QUALIFICATION.md` section 13 bars production qualification while they remain. SA-09 and SA-11 contradict supported behavior, so the disposition was to fix them rather than accept them as limitations.

## Scope and exclusions

Bounded limits that fail with a document exception before the destination grows. No parser redesign. Each trigger becomes a regression test that runs bounded, without destabilizing the test process.

## Acceptance criteria

Each trigger is refused or handled within a stated bound. Every supported fixture still passes. Limits are documented.

## Evidence required

Internally tested.

## Closeout

Accepted at the internally tested level. Closes SA-01, SA-02, SA-03, SA-04, SA-09, and SA-11. G8 is not passed: the fuzz run and the remaining High findings, SA-05, SA-06, and SA-07 (W-37, W-38), were still open. New limits, documented in `README.md` and a new Limits section of `ACCEPTANCE.md`:

- Names, numbers, and keywords stop at 65,536 bytes, and every string branch respects the 32 MiB cap.
- Arrays and dictionaries hold at most 1,048,576 entries, and dictionary parsing is linear.
- Xref streams list object numbers below 8,388,608, with `/Index` subsections ascending without overlap.
- A file lists at most `min(2,097,152, max(65,536, length/8))` distinct objects.
- Object-stream `/N` and `/First` are validated first, and overlapping objects are refused past a work budget of 4 times the decoded length plus 64 KiB.
- The page tree nests at most 256 levels.
- An omitted `/StrF` or `/StmF` in revisions 4 to 6 is `Identity`.
- A malformed `/Filter` or `/DecodeParms` is refused.

QA-B36-01 is now refused after 25 MB under a 256 MiB heap limit. `HardeningTests.cs` holds 34 cases. The full suite passed 269/269 on Windows `net8.0`, `net6.0`, and `net48`, and on Linux `net8.0` and `net6.0` (`make docker-builder docker-test`). Limitations: an indirect `/Filter` is refused (W-40). A classic xref section is built before the budget applies (QA-B36-02, fixed in W-41). Object-stream body allocation churn was recorded as W-42.
