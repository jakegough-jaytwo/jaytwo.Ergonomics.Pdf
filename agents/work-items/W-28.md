# W-28. Bound the seven confirmed parser paths

## Problem

The W-26 review confirmed seven parser defects from the code. None were executed. Comment skipping, `/Prev` chains, cyclic `/Length` and object streams, `Resolve` reference cycles, `WalkField`, and `WalkNames` can recurse or loop without a depth cap or a seen set. A malformed xref `/Index`, xref field width, or object-stream header throws `IndexOutOfRangeException`, `FormatException`, or `OverflowException` instead of `PdfParseException`.

## Why now

Those paths are confirmed, and a bounded fix was the next product step. G8 stays unpassed.

## Scope and exclusions

One bounded fix in `PdfParser.cs`, `PdfEngine.cs`, and the tests that fail today: depth caps and seen sets for the six recursive paths, and `PdfParseException` for the malformed xref `/Index`, xref field width, and object-stream headers. No parser rewrite; a major redesign would be an owner decision. Do not pass G8. Does not replace W-18.

## Acceptance criteria

Each of the seven confirmed paths has a regression test that expects `PdfParseException` or the existing documented refusal, and the file is not extended. `QUALIFICATION.md` section 10. G8 is not passed.

## Evidence required

Internally tested. Not a gate pass.
