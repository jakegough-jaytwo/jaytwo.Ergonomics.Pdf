# W-143. Form fill and flatten workflow operations

## Problem

Filling and flattening forms are the main reason for the library to exist (spec W1 to W3), and stage R2 needs them as workflow operations.

## Scope and exclusions

Add fill (single and bulk) and flatten (all or named) on W-142's pipeline, with the ordering, conflict rules, run result, and signature opt-in that W-141's decision chooses. The engine keeps its all-or-nothing validation and certification, FieldMDP, permission, and usage-rights rules. The workflow composes them and does not re-implement them.

Excluded: any change to engine fill, appearance, or flatten behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

Refine these criteria from W-141's decision at activation.

- A fill workflow and a fill-then-flatten workflow each append one revision whose bytes equal the direct-engine sequence.
- Each conflict the decision lists (for example, filling a field that the workflow also flattens) is rejected before the destination is written, or is resolved by its stated rule.
- A refused fill (an unknown field, a read-only field, a bad value) leaves the destination unchanged and reports the engine's failure category.
- Flatten on a document with an approval signature requires the decision's opt-in, and without it the run fails before output.
- The run result reports fill dispositions as the decision specifies.

## Evidence required

Focused `jaytwo.Ergonomics.Pdf.Tests` on net10.0 and `make quiet-build`.
