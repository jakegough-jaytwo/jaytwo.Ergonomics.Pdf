# W-143. Form fill and flatten workflow operations

## Problem

Filling and flattening forms are the main reason for the library to exist (spec W1 to W3), and stage R2 needs them as workflow operations.

## Scope and exclusions

Implement spec F5, F6, F10 for flatten, and F13's `FieldAppearances` (section 6.7, D-60): `Fill`, `FillForm`, `FlattenForm()`, `FlattenForm(names)`, and `AllowBreakingExistingSignatures()`. The engine keeps its all-or-nothing validation and its certification, FieldMDP, permission, and usage-rights rules. The workflow composes them and does not re-implement them.

Excluded: any change to engine fill, appearance, or flatten behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- F5: fills declared after a flatten still apply before it. Two fills of one field send only the last value, which a test confirms through the result and the filled value. Fills declared in separate calls reach the engine as one bulk fill: one bad value among them leaves the destination unwritten. `FlattenForm("A")` plus `FlattenForm("B")` flattens both fields in one engine call, and `FlattenForm("A")` plus `FlattenForm()` flattens every field.
- F4 and F6: fill and fill-then-flatten workflows each append one revision, keep the input as an exact prefix, and inspect the same as the direct-engine sequence (values, remaining fields, revision count). Where no clock-dependent value is written, the bytes are identical.
- F12: an unknown field, a read-only field, or a bad value throws its engine exception type, and the destination stays unwritten. So does a fill the engine cannot draw followed by a flatten (`PdfUnsupportedFormException`, from `/NeedAppearances`).
- F10: flatten on a document with an approval signature throws `PdfSignatureConflictException` before output without `AllowBreakingExistingSignatures()` and succeeds with it. A certified document stays refused with it.
- F13: `FieldAppearances` holds one disposition for each filled field's final value.

## Evidence required

Focused `jaytwo.Ergonomics.Pdf.Tests` on net10.0 and `make quiet-build`.
