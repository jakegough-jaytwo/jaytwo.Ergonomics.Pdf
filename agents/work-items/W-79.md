# W-79. G7 large-file workloads for Phase 1

## Problem

`QUALIFICATION.md` G7 adds workloads the suite does not run yet: a 1 GiB compressed attachment, a 500-field bulk fill, a 1,000-page flatten, inspection and revision listing on 1 GiB, repeated use and disposal, and encrypted xref-stream discovery.

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 21.

## Scope and exclusions

- The G7 workloads, owner-approved budgets for each new one, and a recorded timing baseline.
- Excluded: throughput targets.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every G7 workload runs within its approved budget on every target.

Refine these criteria, and the `ACCEPTANCE.md` rows they add or change, when the item becomes active. A write item also meets every check in plan C4.

## Evidence required

Release-build measurements with process isolation.
