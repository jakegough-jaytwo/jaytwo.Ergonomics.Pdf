# W-79. G7 large-file workloads for Phase 1

## Problem

`QUALIFICATION.md` G7 adds workloads the suite does not run yet: a 1 GiB compressed attachment, a 500-field bulk fill, a 1,000-page flatten, inspection and revision listing on 1 GiB, repeated use and disposal, and encrypted xref-stream discovery.

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 21.

## Scope and exclusions

- `LargeWorkloadTests` gains the six G7 workloads the suite does not run, on real file-backed inputs, each measured by `WorkloadMeter` and asserted against its D-20 ceiling. The existing dense, attachment, and 500-page tests are extended where the G7 row names the same input.
- A `make g7-workloads` target runs each workload in its own Release process with coverage off, one framework at a time, so the process-wide heap counter sees one workload.
- `agents/evidence/qualification/memory-budgets.md` records the isolated measurements and the timing baseline, and the owner-approved budget for each new workload.
- `ACCEPTANCE.md` gains the Scale rows below.
- Excluded: throughput targets, the sparse 3 GiB row (already `ScaleTests`), and output and decompression limits (already Limits rows). No production code changes; a workload that fails its ceiling is a defect recorded here and split into its own item.

## Acceptance criteria

New `ACCEPTANCE.md` Scale rows, each from `LargeWorkloadTests` on `net10.0`, `net8.0`, `net6.0`, and `net48`:

1. On the signed 1 GiB dense file, `Signatures` and `Revisions` each report the signature covering the edited revision while reading less than 16 MiB of the file, so no signed range is read.
2. A payload of at least 1 GiB is added with `Compress = true` and extracted through file streams. The stored stream is `/FlateDecode` and smaller than the payload, the extracted bytes hash to the payload, the carrier's original bytes are a prefix, and the add reads the payload in requests of at most 64 KiB.
3. On the 500-page object-stream form, one `Fill` of 500 text fields and its save, then a signature over the filled file. The values read back, the signature covers the filled revision, and `qpdf --check` passes.
4. On a 1,000-page object-stream form, after a bulk fill, `Flatten` of every field and its save. No field remains, the page count holds, and `qpdf --check` passes.
5. Twenty open, field-read, and dispose cycles on the 500-page object-stream form, and twenty field reads on one open document, leave the retained managed heap within 1 MiB of the heap after the warm-up cycle.
6. A qpdf AES-256 copy of the 1 GiB dense file, with a cross-reference stream and object streams, opens and is inspected under the user and the owner password while reading less than 16 MiB of the file.
7. Every new measurement window stays within its D-20 ceiling (256 MiB for inspect, edit, and sign; 512 MiB for traversals), and within the owner-approved fixture budget in isolated Release runs.

## Evidence required

Release-build measurements with process isolation.

## Notes

- 2026-10-20: Created under W-63 from `agents/reference/charter-reset-plan.md` C2.
- 2026-10-22: Activated after W-42 and W-50. `LargeWorkloadTests` now runs the six workloads (criteria 1 to 6), `make g7-workloads` runs each test in its own Release process, `ACCEPTANCE.md` has the Scale rows, and acceptance-matrix rows 83 to 89 record the runs. Isolated Release runs passed 24/24 on Windows (`net10.0`, `net8.0`, `net6.0`, `net48`) and 18/18 in the Linux builder image (`net10.0`, `net8.0`, `net6.0`). The Jenkins-equivalent `make quiet-docker-test` passed every W-79 case, and failed only the known 10-second `HardeningTests` timing case, which passed alone. Measurements, the timing baseline, and observations are in `agents/evidence/qualification/memory-budgets.md`.
- 2026-10-22: Two test bugs found and fixed in the first run: `PdfBuilder.Basic()` has no page `/Resources`, so `qpdf --check` warned on the attachment carrier, and `SignedRevisionEnd` is the signed file's end, not the signed-over revision's.
- 2026-10-22: Observations, no defect. (1) Sign and flatten on object-stream forms re-read the source 142 and 410 times its size, because the engine keeps one clean object stream decoded; that is the memory bound working as designed, costing under 0.6 s here. (2) Linux `net6.0` object-stream windows report 65 to 81 MiB peaks that are uncollected garbage under .NET 6's default gen0 budget; a 16 MiB gen0 gives 16 to 25 MiB. (3) Under coverage, the compressed 1 GiB attachment test adds about 5 minutes to the Jenkins run.
- 2026-10-22: Open, owner decision. Criterion 7 needs the owner to approve the fixture budgets for the new workloads, and to decide how the Linux `net6.0` garbage-inflated peaks count against them and against the B-17 500-page budget of 32 MiB. The proposals are in `memory-budgets.md`. The item stays active until then.
