# W-10. Evidence that the 3 GiB metadata appendix stays under 8 KiB

## Problem

The scale row in `ACCEPTANCE.md` says the metadata appendix of the 3 GiB save is under 8 KiB. `three_gigabyte_pdf_is_opened_edited_and_signed_without_reading_the_middle` did not measure that appendix, and the writer has no 8 KiB check. This is W-03 clause 4.

## Why now

The W-05 Core audit and W-03 found an acceptance clause with no assertion.

## Scope and exclusions

## Acceptance criteria

The metadata appendix of the 3 GiB save is asserted to be under 8 KiB, and the existing hole and byte-read checks still pass.
