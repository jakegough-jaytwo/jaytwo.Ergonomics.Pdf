# W-13. Grow the foreign corpus for G2

## Problem

The recorded corpus has six committed foreign PDFs from three producers. Gate G2 asks for at least 50 fixtures from four producers, including a real-world file not built for this library.

## Why now

G2 is release-blocking. The current corpus is the starting point, not the gate.

## Scope and exclusions

Add distinct, meaningful fixtures until the counts are met, covering the structures and supported operations named by G2. Do not pad the corpus with trivial variations. Count a tool as an independent producer only when it authors documents; transformers and signers are recorded separately. Do not use owner documents unless a specific real-world gap requires and authorizes one.

## Acceptance criteria

At least 50 fixtures from at least four independent producers, including one real-world file. Supported fixtures pass applicable checks, deliberately unsupported cases fail predictably, and modified output passes `qpdf --check`.

## Evidence required

Independently validated evidence naming each tool, version, and established property, as required by `QUALIFICATION.md` section 4.
