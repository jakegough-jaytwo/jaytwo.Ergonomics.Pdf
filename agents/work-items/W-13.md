# W-13. Grow the foreign corpus for G2

## Problem

The corpus has six committed foreign PDFs from three producers. Revised G2 asks for at least 30 distinct, meaningful files, at least 5 in each of four categories (business and government forms, Office and browser exports, scans and linearized files, generated reports), two thirds from tools other than the repository's test generators, and a manifest entry for each.

## Why now

G2 is release-blocking. The current corpus is the starting point, not the gate.

## Scope and exclusions

- Fixtures, provenance, licenses, the manifest and its validator, and format probes only. Feature-specific API tests come with the feature items.
- A file that cannot be committed is fetched by a script with a pinned hash; its checks report not run when it is unavailable.
- Do not pad the corpus with trivial variants. Do not use owner documents unless a specific gap requires and authorizes one.

## Acceptance criteria

The revised G2 corpus thresholds are met; every manifest entry validates; supported files pass the existing operations' checks; unsupported files fail predictably; modified output passes `qpdf --check`.

## Evidence required

Independently validated evidence naming each tool, version, and established property, as required by `QUALIFICATION.md` section 4.
