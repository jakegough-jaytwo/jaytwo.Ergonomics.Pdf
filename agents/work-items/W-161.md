# W-161. New-file writer and typed copier foundation

## Problem

Page assembly needs a forward-only writer and a copier that follows typed per-structure policy, and the engine has neither (spec section 2, A12, A13).

## Scope and exclusions

Build the internal new-file writer: object-number allocation, the source-qualified old-to-new map with a registered-before-children rule, raw stream copy, indirect `/Length`, the 64-way page tree, header selection, and classic or stream cross-reference output. Add the budgets and the resource-limit error. Excluded: any public assembly API, page selection, links, forms, and encryption.

## Acceptance criteria

- A12: the header is at least 1.5 and follows the effective version. A classic table is written when every offset fits ten digits and a cross-reference stream otherwise, tested at the boundary. No object stream is written.
- A13: mapped objects, depth, output pages, and structural memory each have a limit that raises the dedicated error.
- Raw copy keeps the encoded bytes of a Flate stream and an image stream unchanged and never allocates the stream length; a large sparse stream copies in bounded memory.
- A cycle in the copied graph ends. A destination object is registered before its children.
- The output opens in qpdf and Poppler and passes `qpdf --check`.

## Evidence required

Focused `jaytwo.Pdf.Tests` on net10.0 and `make quiet-build`. The independent-tool check runs once at the item's end, not per task.
