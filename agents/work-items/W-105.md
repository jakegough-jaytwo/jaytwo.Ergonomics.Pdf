# W-105. Refuse signed hybrid XFA fills and drop /UR3 on unsigned hybrid fills

## Problem

W-76 removes `/XFA` on every hybrid fill and keeps `/UR3` under `/Form FillIn`. On a signed hybrid form, pyHanko 0.38.0 then reports the earlier signature's document as modified beyond form filling (`OTHER`, not permitted, citing `/XFA`). Nothing shows `FillIn` permits deleting the XFA definition. D-48 decides both.

## Why now

The current behavior knowingly produces revisions that validators reject on signed forms, and it keeps usage rights it cannot show permit the write.

## Scope and exclusions

- A fill or bulk fill of a signed hybrid form throws `PdfSignatureConflictException`. The exception becomes public API in the root namespace, as spec 7.2 names it, so W-77 and W-78 reuse it.
- A fill of an unsigned hybrid form removes `/XFA` and `/UR3` in the same revision.
- Excluded: an `AllowBreakingSignatures` opt-in for fills (D-48); flatten (W-77) and rotate (W-78), which apply 7.2 themselves; XFA-only forms, which are unchanged.

## Proposed spec wording (owner approval required before activation, D-47)

- W4, status Change: "**Hybrid XFA.** The first fill of an unsigned hybrid form removes `/XFA` from the AcroForm dictionary in the same revision, so every viewer shows the AcroForm values; it also removes `/UR3` (section 7.3). A fill of a hybrid form that has a signature throws `PdfSignatureConflictException`. A write to an XFA-only form throws `PdfUnsupportedFormException`."
- 7.2, second bullet, last sentence: "Fills, metadata, attachments, and new signatures stay allowed, as today, except a fill of a hybrid XFA form, which throws `PdfSignatureConflictException` on a document with any signature (W4)."
- 7.3, after the list of grants: "A fill that removes `/XFA` (W4) is not granted by `FillIn`, which permits filling fields, not deleting the XFA definition, so it removes `/UR3`."

## Acceptance criteria

1. On a hybrid form with a certification (`P` 2 or `P` 3), an approval signature, or a document timestamp, a fill and a non-empty bulk fill throw `PdfSignatureConflictException` and leave `/XFA`, `/UR3`, and the document unchanged; a later save writes nothing. `P` 1 still throws `PdfCertificationLockedException`. A signature field whose `/V` does not resolve and a `/UR3` signature alone do not make the form signed.
2. On the pyHanko-approved and `P` 2-certified IRS W-9 (`usage_rights_samples.py`), a fill throws and the source bytes are unchanged; pyHanko still reports the signature intact and valid.
3. A fill of an unsigned hybrid form removes `/XFA` and `/UR3` in the same revision, whatever the transform grants; the IRS W-9 and VA 21-4138 corpus fill and bulk fill lose `/UR3` as pypdf reads them, and `qpdf --check` passes. A fill of a form without XFA keeps `/UR3` under `FillIn`, as before.
4. Plan C4: an encrypted unsigned hybrid form with `/UR3`, under the user and the owner password, loses `/XFA` and `/UR3` and keeps the prefix, `/Encrypt`, and `/ID`; qpdf and pypdf read it with the password. Refusals do not dirty the document.
5. Fill then `Sign` in one session on an unsigned hybrid form: the fill removes `/XFA` and `/UR3`, and the signature applies, since the signed check runs before `Sign` adds its own signature.
6. `ACCEPTANCE.md` rows for XFA and usage rights, `README.md`, and spec W4, 7.2, and 7.3 (approved wording) are updated; W4 returns to Exists in the same commit (D-43).

## Evidence required

pyHanko on the signed W-9; qpdf and pypdf on corpus and encrypted output.
