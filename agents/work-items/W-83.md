# W-83. Let one commit activate and complete an item

## Problem

`make task-check` accepted a completion to `done` only from `active` or `ready`. W-40 went from `proposed` to `done` and had to split into a work commit and a record-only close commit.

## Why now

The owner asked on 2026-10-20 that one commit be able to both activate and complete an item.

## Scope and exclusions

- `tools/agents/workflow.py`: a staged `done` owns the transaction unless the item was already `done` or `cancelled` at `HEAD`.
- Tests for `proposed` to `done`, a new item created `done`, a `cancelled` item that cannot own work, and a staged-index transaction that activates, completes, and carries work.
- D-42 records the change; `AGENTS.md` names it.
- Excluded: every other `task-check` and `agents-check` rule.

## Acceptance criteria

- A commit that moves an item from an open status to `done` with work inside its `allowed_changes` passes `make task-check`. No `ACCEPTANCE.md` row changes.

## Evidence required

`python -m unittest test_workflow` in `tools/agents`, `make agents-check`, and `make task-check`. This commit is itself a new item created `done` with its work.
