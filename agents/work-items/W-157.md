# W-157. Reduce cryptographic material lifetime

## Problem

File keys, derived key buffers, and cryptographic objects may survive longer than the document or operation that owns them, including on failure paths.

## Scope and exclusions

Inventory secret-bearing buffers and cryptographic disposables, give each a clear owner and lifetime, clear buffers where practical, and repair failure-path disposal. Do not add a runtime dependency or claim complete erasure from managed memory.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each secret-bearing buffer with a defined lifecycle is cleared when its owner is disposed or the operation ends, including tested failure paths.
- Cryptographic objects are disposed exactly once on success and failure.
- Tests use internal seams or instrumented owners to prove cleanup without depending on nondeterministic garbage collection.
- Public documentation, if changed, describes the best-effort managed-memory limitation accurately.

## Evidence required

Focused encryption and signing lifecycle tests on a current target and net48, plus a warning-free build of affected projects.
