# W-149. Decide cumulative work budgets for document operations

## Problem

Existing per-stream bounds do not establish a cumulative computational-work bound for one operation. Repeated object-stream decoding, stacked filters, repeated traversal, and gaps in cancellation may amplify work on a hostile document.

## Scope and exclusions

Instrument representative benign and adversarial fixtures and measure decoded bytes, repeated decoding, traversal, and cancellation latency per public operation. Compare cumulative-budget and checkpoint strategies while preserving large-file support and operation isolation. Do not implement a limit or operation-local cache in this item.

## Acceptance criteria

- Measurements identify the material amplification paths and distinguish source I/O, decoded bytes, traversal work, and retained memory.
- A proposal defines the counter scope, reset boundary, limit behavior, exception and cancellation behavior, and compatibility impact.
- The proposal explains why any cache is or is not justified by evidence.
- The owner can select a strategy; the selected decision creates narrowly scoped implementation and regression-test work items.

## Evidence required

A reproducible measurement summary over existing benign large-workload fixtures and purpose-built adversarial fixtures, with commands and observed counts recorded in this item or a referenced decision proposal.
