# W-76. Classify XFA forms and remove hybrid XFA on fill

## Problem

Filling a hybrid AcroForm and XFA form looks lost in Acrobat, which shows the XFA data (spec R12, W4).

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 17. It adds XFA detection, an input surface, before the G8 campaign.

## Scope and exclusions

- `IsHybridXfa` and `IsXfaOnly`.
- The first form write to a hybrid form removes `/XFA`.
- A write to an XFA-only form throws `PdfUnsupportedFormException`.
- "Form write" is a fill (W1, W2) here. Flatten (W3, W-77) does not exist yet; W-77 applies W4 when it adds flatten. Signing, metadata, attachment, and rotate writes are not form writes (spec 6.1 lists W4 under Forms) and are unchanged.
- Excluded: XFA editing, reading or decoding the XFA packets, and the second renderer (W-80).

## Acceptance criteria

1. R12. A form has XFA when the AcroForm `/XFA` resolves to a stream or an array (ISO 32000-1 Table 218). It is XFA-only when it has XFA and the catalog `/NeedsRendering` is true or it has no field widgets; it is hybrid when it has XFA and is neither. Without XFA both are false. Classification never decodes an XFA stream. On the corpus, W-9 and VA 21-4138 are hybrid, SF-424 is XFA-only, and every other file is neither, in agreement with the corpus probe's `xfa` feature (pypdf).
2. W4, hybrid. A fill or bulk fill that applies removes `/XFA` from the AcroForm dictionary in the same revision, so the reopened document and pypdf see no `/XFA`, and `IsHybridXfa` is false afterwards. A refused fill (validation, permission bits, DocMDP, FieldMDP) and an empty bulk fill keep `/XFA` and do not dirty the document. On W-9 and VA 21-4138, the corpus fill and bulk fill pass `qpdf --check` and the probe reads the values with no `xfa` feature; usage rights follow the existing spec 7.3 `FillIn` rule.
3. W4, XFA-only. A fill and a bulk fill throw `PdfUnsupportedFormException` before any other check and leave the document clean: a later save writes nothing. Reads still work: pages, metadata, attachments, an empty `Fields` and `Export`. SF-424 refuses a fill.
4. Rendering (G6). Poppler renders the filled value on W-9 and VA 21-4138 after the fill removes `/XFA`. The second renderer is W-80, whose criterion is that every G6 render check runs in both renderers, so it includes this one.
5. Plan C4. An encrypted hybrid form (AES-256 and RC4) filled under the user and the owner password removes `/XFA`, keeps the original bytes as a prefix and `/Encrypt` and `/ID`, and is read by pypdf and `qpdf --check` with the password; a user password without the fill bit refuses and keeps `/XFA`. DocMDP `P` 1 refuses a fill of a hybrid form and keeps `/XFA`; `P` 2 allows it and removes `/XFA`; a FieldMDP lock refuses and keeps `/XFA`. pyHanko's verdict on the earlier signature of a signed hybrid form after the fill is measured and recorded in `ACCEPTANCE.md`.
6. `ACCEPTANCE.md` gains the R12 and W4 rows and corrects the usage-rights row that lists SF-424 among forms that keep `/UR3` after a fill. `README.md` documents the shipped behavior. Spec R12 and W4 become Exists (D-43).

## Evidence required

Corpus forms; qpdf and pypdf structural reads; Poppler rendering (MuPDF through W-80); pyHanko for the signed case.

## Notes

- 2026-10-20: Created under W-63 from `agents/reference/charter-reset-plan.md` C2.

Poppler and MuPDF both ignore XFA, so the structural reads (no `/XFA` after the fill) are the evidence that XFA-aware viewers fall back to the AcroForm values; the renders show those values are drawn.
- 2026-10-21: Design. `PdfEngine.Fill` classifies once, against the same field walk the checks use. XFA-only throws before the certification, FieldMDP, and permission checks, so it never dirties the document. The hybrid removal runs inside the existing restore scope, after every check and before the fills, so a later failure restores `/XFA` with the other edits. `/NeedsRendering` makes a form XFA-only even when it has widgets: viewers that ignore XFA then draw only the placeholder page content, so removing `/XFA` would leave nothing usable. The fuzz harness reads both properties on every input, outside its `HasFields` guard.
- 2026-10-21: Measured, pyHanko 0.38.0 on the IRS W-9 approved or certified (`P` 2) by `usage_rights_samples.py`, then filled: the earlier signature is intact and valid, modification `OTHER`, `docmdp_ok` false, and the suspicious-modification reason names `/XFA`. pyHanko's `ACROFORM_EXEMPT_STRICT_COMPARISON` lists `/Fields`, `/DR`, `/DA`, `/Q`, and `/NeedAppearances`, not `/XFA`; it holds an approval-only document to `P` 2. W4 is unconditional, so the fill follows it, and `ACCEPTANCE.md` and `README.md` record the limitation as row 68 does for attachments. Owner question 1 below.
- 2026-10-21: Owner questions, not decided here:
  1. **Signed hybrid forms.** Spec 7.2 lets `P` 2 and `P` 3 certifications and approval signatures accept fills, and pyHanko reports such fills as `FORM_FILLING` on forms without XFA. On a signed hybrid form the W4 removal turns that into a change pyHanko does not permit; Acrobat was not run. Options: keep W4 as written (current); keep `/XFA` once the form has a signature (Acrobat may then show the stale XFA data); or refuse a fill of a signed hybrid form. Changing it is a spec and certification-guarantee decision.
  2. **Usage rights.** A fill keeps `/UR3` under `/Form FillIn` (spec 7.3), and the same revision now also removes `/XFA`. Table 255's `FillIn` does not say whether it covers removing the XFA definition, and no Acrobat or Reader check was run. The current rule is unchanged.
