# W-76. Classify XFA forms and remove hybrid XFA on fill

## Problem

Filling a hybrid AcroForm and XFA form looks lost in Acrobat, which shows the XFA data (spec R12, W4).

## Why now

Reset plan C2 item 17. It adds XFA detection, an input surface, before the G8 campaign.

## Scope and exclusions

- `IsHybridXfa` and `IsXfaOnly`.
- The first form write to a hybrid form removes `/XFA`.
- A write to an XFA-only form throws `PdfUnsupportedFormException`.
- Excluded: XFA editing.

## Acceptance criteria

- Corpus hybrid forms show the filled values in two renderers.
- XFA-only forms refuse writes and still read.

Refine these criteria, and the `ACCEPTANCE.md` rows they add or change, when the item becomes active. A write item also meets every check in plan C4.

## Evidence required

Corpus forms; two renderers.
