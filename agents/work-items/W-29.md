# W-29. Bound the two confirmed signer paths

## Problem

The W-27 review confirmed two signer defects from the code. Neither input was executed. `HttpTimestampAuthority.ExtractToken` treats a negative PKI status as success, because `ReadInteger` folds a 4-byte INTEGER such as `02 04 80 00 00 00` into a signed value that is not greater than 1. `DerCodec.ReadTlv` adds `index + length` in an unchecked `int`, so a long-form length of `0x7FFFFFFF` wraps the bounds check and `Slice` throws `ArgumentOutOfRangeException`.

## Why now

Those paths are confirmed, and a bounded fix was the next product step. G8 stays unpassed.

## Scope and exclusions

Bound the two paths in `DerCodec.cs` and `HttpTimestampAuthority.cs`, with regression tests. Do not reopen the nonce, imprint, or throwing-signer work (W-04, W-19). Do not pass G8. Does not replace W-18. `SignatureTests.cs` waited until W-25 released it.

## Acceptance criteria

- A negative PKI status throws `PdfTimestampException` and does not extend the file.
- A long-form DER length that would wrap the bounds check throws `PdfTimestampException`, not `ArgumentOutOfRangeException`, and does not extend the file.
- `QUALIFICATION.md` section 10. G8 is not passed.

## Evidence required

Internally tested. Not a gate pass.
