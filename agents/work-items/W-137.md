# W-137. Split PdfEngine into responsibility-sized internal types

## Problem

`Internal/PdfEngine.cs` is 5,687 lines. It holds the cross-reference load and object cache, edit tracking and backup, the page tree, information and XMP metadata, form fill and appearances, flatten, attachments and name trees, certification, permissions and usage rights, signature inspection and signing, and the incremental writer. The owner asked for the parser to be split for maintainability (W-135); the engine is the larger instance of the same problem.

## Scope and exclusions

Behavior-preserving split under `csharp-refactoring`, by verbatim moves:

- At activation, map the clusters and their shared state, then choose seams where state ownership is clear. Candidates: the open path and object store (xref chain, `Load`, `Resolve`, object streams, the cache, and the D-49 lease), edit tracking and rollback, and the incremental writer, with forms, flatten, metadata, attachments, certification and permissions, and signatures as collaborators over them.
- Prefer separate internal types with explicit dependencies. Use `partial` only where a cluster cannot yet leave the engine's private state, and record why.
- The document and editor keep their public surface. Bytes written, exception types, messages, offsets, and the D-49 and D-20 numbers do not change.

Excluded: behavior changes, algorithm rewrites (`reset-plan.md` migration rule 6), and the async inspection of W-138.

## Acceptance criteria

- No file in the split exceeds about 1,000 lines, and each holds one responsibility.
- The build passes with StyleCop warnings as errors on every target, and the output-identity fixtures, D-49 tripwires, and G7 budgets pass unchanged.

## Evidence required

`make quiet-test LANE=fast`, the `Sweep` and `LargeData` lanes on net10.0, and the `Corpus` and `Interop` lanes on net10.0, since the engine is under every operation.

## Closeout

Split `PdfEngine.cs` (5,687 lines) by moving each member verbatim into the type that owns its state. `PdfEngine` (303 lines) opens a document and forwards each internal entry point to `PdfObjectStore` (831; source, cursor, xref map, cache, dirty set, backup, trailer values, version raise, D-49 lease), `PdfDocumentLoader` (465; header, startxref, xref chain, trailer, security), `PdfWriteGate` (562; permissions, DocMDP, FieldMDP, usage rights, signature state), `PdfPageTree` (131), `PdfInfoMetadata` (328), `PdfFieldTree` (462), `PdfFormAppearances` (309), `PdfFormFiller` (521), `PdfFlattener` (580), `PdfEmbeddedFiles` (450), `PdfSignatureInspector` (277), `PdfIncrementalWriter` (360), or `PdfSigner` (389). The shared static helpers are in `PdfValues`, and `FieldSlot`, `PageInfo`, `XfaKind`, `RevisionSection`, `AppendixLayout`, `WrittenObject`, and `SignatureState` are now top-level types. Engine fields that other types read became properties of their owner; `_id` is `PdfObjectStore.TrailerId`, and `_signatureState` is `PdfWriteGate.CachedSignatureState`. `MaxClassicOffset` moved to `PdfIncrementalWriter`. The internal surface that `PdfDocument`, the editor, and the tests use is unchanged.

The only edits beyond qualifiers and accessibility: `Restore` no longer takes the Helvetica id and the sticky `/NeedAppearances` flag. Fill restores them itself. Flatten and sign no longer save them, because nothing on their paths changes them. `BeginBackup` and `EndBackup` replace direct writes of the backup map. `PdfAttachment` receives the engine through `PdfEmbeddedFiles`. A script compared every moved member with the original, with the added qualifiers removed and the old field names restored; it found no other difference. The SA1201, SA1202, and SA1204 pragma stays only on the 12 files that keep the engine's interleaved member order. `PdfEngine`, `PdfPageTree`, and `PdfValues` build without it. The fuzz comments name the new owners of `ParseObjectStreamAsync` and `SyncXmp`. The code map lists the new files and corrects its stale target frameworks.

Verification: `make quiet-build` and the fuzz project built with 0 errors on every target, with StyleCop warnings as errors. `make quiet-test LANE=fast` passed 3,944/3,944 on net10.0, net8.0, net6.0, and net48, and in the netstandard2.0 lane on net8.0. `make quiet-test TEST_FRAMEWORK=net10.0 TEST_FILTER="Category=Sweep|Category=LargeData|Category=Corpus|Category=Interop"` passed 486/486. The output-identity fixtures, the D-49 tripwires, and the G7 budgets are in those runs and are unchanged.
