# W-137. Split PdfEngine into responsibility-sized internal types

## Problem

`Internal/PdfEngine.cs` is 5,687 lines. It holds the cross-reference load and object cache, edit tracking and backup, the page tree, information and XMP metadata, form fill and appearances, flatten, attachments and name trees, certification, permissions and usage rights, signature inspection and signing, and the incremental writer. The owner asked for the parser to be split for maintainability (W-135); the engine is the larger instance of the same problem.

## Scope and exclusions

Behavior-preserving split under `csharp-refactoring`, by verbatim moves:

- At activation, map the clusters and their shared state, then choose seams where state ownership is clear. Candidates: the open path and object store (xref chain, `Load`, `Resolve`, object streams, the cache, and the D-49 lease), edit tracking and rollback, and the incremental writer, with forms, flatten, metadata, attachments, certification and permissions, and signatures as collaborators over them.
- Prefer separate internal types with explicit dependencies. Use `partial` only where a cluster cannot yet leave the engine's private state, and record why.
- The document and editor keep their public surface. Bytes written, exception types, messages, offsets, and the D-49 and D-20 numbers do not change.

Excluded: behavior changes, algorithm rewrites (`reset-plan.md` migration rule 6), and the async inspection of W-138.

## Acceptance criteria

- No file in the split exceeds about 1,000 lines, and each holds one responsibility.
- The build passes with StyleCop warnings as errors on every target, and the output-identity fixtures, D-49 tripwires, and G7 budgets pass unchanged.

## Evidence required

`make quiet-test LANE=fast`, the `Sweep` and `LargeData` lanes on net10.0, and the `Corpus` and `Interop` lanes on net10.0, since the engine is under every operation.
