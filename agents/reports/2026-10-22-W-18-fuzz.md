## Limitations

- Elapsed wall time multiplied by worker count is an estimate, not measured process CPU time.
- The runner did not set or record an explicit libFuzzer PRNG seed.
- SharpFuzz instruments this library, not the BCL XML parser. The `xmp` target stayed at coverage 55 with a seven-input corpus, so it is weak evidence for the parser inside `XDocument.Load`; its structured seeds and mutation volume still exercise the library's surrounding failure path.
- `der` plateaued at coverage 147 and a 61-input corpus. The target completed its allocation without an artifact, but the plateau limits what the run establishes.
- The runner's generated-seed hash excludes committed regression inputs; their separate counts and hashes are recorded above.
