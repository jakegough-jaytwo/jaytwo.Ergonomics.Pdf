# F-2: check-box widgets with different on-state names

## Task

Reproduce and triage skill-review finding F-2. If confirmed High, fix it before the B-18 campaign.

## Defect confirmed

One check-box field had two widget annotations. Their `/AP /N` dictionaries used `First` and `Second` as their respective on-state names. `SetCheckBox("Agree", true)` chose `First` as the field `/V` and wrote `/AS /First` to both widgets. The second widget had no `First` appearance, so the operation reported success while publishing an appearance state the widget could not draw.

Impact: a supported fill operation silently produced an inconsistent check-box field. Severity: High.

## Changes made

- `PdfEngine.SetCheckBox` refuses a checked field when two widgets advertise different non-null on-state names. The check happens before `EditAny`, so the document stays clean.
- `AcroFormTests.check_box_widgets_with_different_on_states_are_rejected_without_writing` constructs the two-widget field, requires `PdfFieldValueException`, saves, and proves the stream length is unchanged.
- `README.md` and `ACCEPTANCE.md` document the refusal.
