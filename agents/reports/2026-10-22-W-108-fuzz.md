## Predeclared plan

- Work item: W-108. Decision: D-49, which requires all eight G8 targets to be rerun from the changed code, because `PdfCursor` sits under every one of them.
- Declared before any target started, after the intermediate W-108 commit that carries the cursor change, the operation lease change, the counters, and the tests.
- Changed code: `PdfCursor` keeps its eight most recently filled 64 KiB windows and serves a seek inside one without I/O; the engine releases them when each public operation returns and at the end of `Open`.
- Targets and allocation: `open`, `edit`, `object`, `objstm`, `decode`, `der`, `tsr`, and `xmp`; 120 seconds per target with 7 workers, the predeclared allocation of the W-18 campaign (`agents/reports/2026-10-22-W-18-fuzz.md`). - Per-input limits: 10 seconds and 2,048 MiB RSS; 1 MiB maximum input for `open` and `edit`, and 64 KiB for every other target.
- Seed sources: harness-generated fixture and builder seeds, qpdf and OpenSSL variants from `tools/fuzz/external-seeds.sh`, and the committed minimized inputs in `tools/fuzz/regressions/`.
- Outcome rule: only the documented exceptions in `.agents/skills/pdf-fuzz-qualification/references/targets.md` are controlled rejections. Every other exception, crash, stack overflow, timeout, or memory-limit breach is an artifact requiring reproduction, deduplication, severity, and disposition.
- Completion rule: every target reaches its allocation and every artifact is triaged. A confirmed defect becomes a deterministic regression and the affected targets are rerun from the fixed code.
- Output directory: `out/fuzz-2026-10-22-w108`.
