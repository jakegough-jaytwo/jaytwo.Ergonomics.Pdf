# D-29. Widen the appearance subset for auto size and `/MK` colors (B-47)


Decided 2026-10-19 by the owner, from F-5. Both parts are approved. A `/DA` font size of zero on a single-line text field also fits the value to the field width, with 6 points as the floor. A regenerated appearance paints `/MK /BG` and `/MK /BC` as a solid border at the `/BS` width. Captions, icons, `/MK /R` rotation, and other border styles stay out of the subset. Any further widening is still an owner decision.
