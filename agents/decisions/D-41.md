# D-41. The agent workflow is slimmed

## Context

The charter reset plan (`agents/reference/charter-reset-plan.md`, section 4) found ceremony and stale guidance in the agent workflow. The skill plan D-27 approved still planned four qualification skills for the pre-Phase 1 scope. `task-check` required a record-only commit to activate an item before its work could be committed.

## Decision

- The tail plan stops. The interop, performance, spec-conformance, and release-qualification skills are cancelled, and `agents/reference/skill-plan.md` is deleted. The durable artifacts they would have produced stay where `QUALIFICATION.md` and the work items put them: the corpus manifest and its validator (G2), the performance workloads and timing baseline (G7), and the evidence inventory with its freshness check and the generated release-gate summary (section 3), all maintained through `pdf-change-verification`. The clause-to-contract map is deferred until a concrete conformance problem justifies it.
- One commit may activate an item and carry its work. `make task-check` reads the staged index. Work-item records and `agents/state/current.md` are record paths and need no owner. Every other staged path belongs to exactly one owner: the item whose activation, or completion from `active` or `ready` to `done`, the transaction stages, or else the item still active from `HEAD`. An ambiguous owner fails. Each such path must fall within the owner's staged `allowed_changes`, and the staged state must pass the same validation as `make agents-check`.
- `pdf-task-workflow` is shortened. The other skills stay.

## Consequences

D-27 and D-32 are superseded. A typical feature task is one activation-plus-work commit, `make agents-check` and `make task-check`, one domain skill plus `pdf-change-verification`, and no report unless it produces qualification evidence.

The owner, 2026-10-20, under W-65.
