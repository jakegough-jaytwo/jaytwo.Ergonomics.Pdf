# D-70. Appearance font scope and retirement of long-term PAdES construction

## Context

W-218's scope reset exposed the older Phase 1 commitments to caller-supplied appearance fonts and PAdES B-LT/B-LTA. The owner explicitly resolved both rather than allowing omission from SUPPORTED.md to cancel them implicitly.

## Decision

- Generate text and choice-field appearances using caller-supplied TrueType fonts, embedded or subset as needed, for a documented character and script subset. Unsupported characters or shaping requirements must produce an explicit outcome.
- Construct detached CMS and PAdES B-B/B-T signatures using host-controlled keys, and append document timestamps. PAdES B-LT/B-LTA construction and cryptographic signature validation are outside scope.
- Retire and explicitly supersede the older planned-for-1.x B-LT/B-LTA commitment. Separate document timestamps alone do not establish B-LTA conformance.

## Consequences

The font operation contract must define its character and script subset and explicit unsupported outcomes before implementation. This scope does not introduce general text layout or document authoring. No delivery milestone or new implementation is adopted here.

The libraries do not take on collecting and embedding certificate/revocation evidence for B-LT/B-LTA construction or maintaining archival timestamp chains. Existing B-B/B-T signing, timestamping, inspection, and host-controlled keys retain their current behavior contracts.

W-218 reconciles SUPPORTED.md, AGENTS.md, the Phase 1 planning text, README, and the planned-additions overview. Historical records retain their original context. Current implementation, acceptance requirements, and qualification evidence are unchanged.

## Authority

Jake explicitly supplied the font scope and approved retirement of both long-term PAdES profiles during the W-218 interview on 2026-10-27.
