# D-48. Refuse fills of signed hybrid XFA forms; an unsigned hybrid fill removes /UR3

## Context

W-76 shipped spec W4 as written: the first fill of a hybrid XFA form removes `/XFA` from the AcroForm dictionary, and the fill keeps `/UR3` under `/Form FillIn` (spec 7.3). It measured that on the IRS W-9, after a pyHanko approval signature or `P` 2 certification, pyHanko 0.38.0 reports the fill as modification `OTHER`, not permitted, naming `/XFA`. It also found that nothing shows `FillIn` (ISO 32000-1 Table 255) permits deleting the XFA definition. It left both points to the owner.

Keeping `/XFA` on a signed form would let Acrobat show stale XFA data silently. Removing it knowingly produces a revision that validators classify as unpermitted.

## Decision

1. A fill or bulk fill of a hybrid XFA form that already has a signature throws `PdfSignatureConflictException` and changes nothing. A document has a signature as spec 7.3 and W-90 define it: the catalog `/Perms` has `/DocMDP`, or a signature field in the AcroForm tree has a `/V` that resolves to a dictionary; the `/UR3` signature does not count. A fill of an unsigned hybrid form still removes `/XFA`.
2. When a fill of an unsigned hybrid form removes `/XFA`, it also removes `/UR3` from `/Perms` in the same revision, whatever the UR3 transform grants: `/Form FillIn` permits filling fields, not deleting the XFA definition. This applies the charter's rule that usage rights are kept only when they provably permit the whole write. A fill of a form without XFA keeps `/UR3` under `FillIn` as before. A refused fill keeps `/UR3`.

The two work together: signed hybrid fills are refused, and unsigned hybrid fills proceed but give up the Reader extensions that cannot be shown to permit them.

## Consequences

- Spec W4, 7.2, and 7.3 change. The text needs the owner's approval of wording (D-47); W-105 proposes it.
- `PdfSignatureConflictException`, which spec 7.2 already names for flatten and rotate, becomes public API with this change.
- The Reader-extended corpus forms IRS W-9 and VA 21-4138 lose `/UR3` after a fill. A pyHanko-signed W-9 refuses a fill instead of producing a revision pyHanko rejects.
- No caller opt-in, such as `AllowBreakingSignatures`, is added for fills. Adding one is a separate owner decision.
- XFA-only forms are unchanged: a fill throws `PdfUnsupportedFormException`.

## Authority

The owner, 2026-10-21, answering W-76's owner questions 1 and 2. Implemented by W-105.
