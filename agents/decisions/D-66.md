# D-66. Prioritize simplification research and retain distinct security obligations

## Context

After W-187 created W-188 through W-211, the owner requested their prioritization in `agents/state/current.md` and an assessment of whether remaining security hardening had been obviated. This is authorization to schedule research; it does not approve the simplification charter's product recommendations.

## Decision

- `agents/state/current.md` owns the detailed order. Retain W-184/W-185/W-186 first, establish the research baseline and corpus, and prioritize assembly preservation/admission choices ahead of cheaper policy/API investigations and optional compatibility-sensitive removals. Keep unaffected encryption research ahead of the longer simplification investigations.
- Schedule W-197 before W-156 and W-210 before W-158. W-156's general host admission API is a possible scope reduction, not a demonstrated replacement: assembly preflight alone does not meet its completeness and policy requirements. W-158's trust-boundary guidance remains necessary even if forensic properties are narrowed.
- Retain W-155, W-168, W-149 and W-159. Supported encryption still needs interoperability evidence; per-stream limits and performance cleanup do not establish cumulative work bounds; hosts still own process, storage and network controls. No remaining security item is cancelled or considered satisfied by a research proposal.
- W-168 keeps its owner-directed, independently reviewed fixture prerequisite and eligibility exception. Do not generate those fixtures as a substitute for the approved task or block unrelated ready research on their absence.
- Research dependencies, source availability and genuinely independent evidence requirements still apply. Queue placement approves research scheduling only. Exact product wording, corresponding implementation tickets and their insertion still require Gate 2 approval; no silent widening of product scope.
- D-62's security exit and R5 boundary remain in force. Completed security records stay completed. Any later cancellation/rescope needs an explicit owner-approved disposition after the research supplies evidence.

## Authority

The owner, 2026-10-26: requested prioritization of the new tickets in current.md and reassessment of security-hardening overlap. The sequence and overlap assessment implement that request; no approval of a product removal, changed guarantee or security-item cancellation is inferred.
