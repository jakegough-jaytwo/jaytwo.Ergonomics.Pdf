# D-26. Unresolvable references are null (B-44)


Decided 2026-10-19 by the owner: follow the spec. Under ISO 32000-1 7.3.10, a reference to an undefined object, a free entry, or a generation other than the xref entry's resolves as the null object. The charter rule stands that a certification that does not resolve is treated as `P` 1, and it extends to null certification and FieldMDP lock data. No general repair heuristics.
