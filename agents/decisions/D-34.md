# D-34. Historical review and role folders leave the tree

## Context

D-25 kept `roles/` and the review rounds in the tree as history so older decisions and reports stayed understandable. After D-25 and D-33, nothing active reads them: no skill, tool, or make target refers to them, and their rationale is already distilled into decisions, `.agents/skills/`, and the agreed skill plan.

## Decision

Remove `agents/roles/`, `agents/reviews/`, `agents/skill-reviews/`, and `agents/workflow-review/`. W-54 records their removal; their pre-removal tree state is [squashed].

The agreed skill plan is still the approved tail plan, so it moves to `agents/reference/skill-plan.md` instead of being removed.

This supersedes D-25's statement that `roles/` remains as history. `team.md`, `archive/`, and `reports/` are unchanged by this decision.

## Consequences

Paths to the removed folders in decisions, reports, archived state, and closed work items identify historical inputs whose pre-removal tree state is [squashed]. D-34 and W-54 preserve their provenance.

## Authority

The owner, 2026-10-19.
