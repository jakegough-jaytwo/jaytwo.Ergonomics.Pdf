# D-42. One commit may activate and complete an item

## Context

D-41 let one commit activate an item and carry its work, but `task-check` accepted a completion only from `active` or `ready`. A `proposed` item finished in one commit was refused, so W-40 needed a second, record-only commit to close it.

## Decision

A transaction owns its work when it activates an item or completes one to `done` from any open status, or from no prior record. A `done` or `cancelled` item at `HEAD` cannot own work. The other D-41 rules stand: one owner, an ambiguous owner fails, and every staged path other than work-item records and `agents/state/current.md` falls within the owner's staged `allowed_changes`.

## Consequences

A task can be one commit that activates the item, carries its work, and closes it. Intermediate commits stay allowed. This amends the owner rule of D-41.

## Authority

The owner, 2026-10-20, under W-83.
