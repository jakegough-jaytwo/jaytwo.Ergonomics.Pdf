# D-35. Role-era team contract, prompts, and reports leave the tree

## Context

D-25 kept `team.md` and the role-named reports as history. D-34 removed the role prompts and review rounds but left `team.md`, `archive/`, and `reports/` in place. Nothing active reads the team contract, the v1 role prompts, the role-named reports, or the four legacy state pointers. Their durable content is already in decisions, `.agents/skills/`, `agents/evidence/`, and `archive/state-v1/`.

## Decision

Remove `agents/team.md`, `agents/archive/roles-v1/`, every role-named report in `agents/reports/` (`*-product-manager-*`, `*-tech-lead-*`, `*-core-engineer-*`, `*-acroform-engineer-*`, `*-signing-engineer-*`, `*-qa-engineer-*`, `*-security-auditor-*`), and the pointer files `agents/state/backlog.md`, `board.md`, `decisions.md`, and `status.md`. W-55 records their removal; their pre-removal tree state is [squashed].

`agents/reports/` remains the location for new reports. Reports written under the single-agent workflow stay. `archive/state-v1/` stays, because D-33 makes it the canonical record of closed B items.

This supersedes D-25's statement that `team.md` and role-named reports remain as history.

## Consequences

Reusable evidence that cited a removed report keeps its path and identifies D-35 and W-55 as the removal record; the report's pre-removal tree state is [squashed]. Paths to removed files in decisions, remaining reports, archived state, and closed work items remain as historical facts.

## Authority

The owner, 2026-10-19.
