# D-50. Decision records commit without an owning item

## Context

D-41 and D-42 exempt work-item records and `agents/state/current.md` from the owner check, so planning can be committed without an active item. Decision records were not exempt. A new decision cited by new work items could not be committed: with the decision staged, the commit needed an owning item; without it, the work items cited a decision that did not exist. On 2026-10-22 this left D-49, W-108, and W-109 uncommitted, and the agent proposed a placeholder item to route around the check.

## Decision

Decision records under `agents/decisions/` are planning records. `task-check` exempts them from the owner's `allowed_changes`, like work-item records and `agents/state/current.md`. A commit that stages only planning records needs no owning item. Staged-state validation still applies, so cross-references must resolve within the staged index.

Planning records are committed as soon as they are consistent. The queue in `agents/state/current.md` authorizes starting the next item without asking. When `task-check` rejects a planning-only commit, the check is fixed under a work item; no item is added to route around it.

## Consequences

Decisions and the items that cite them land together in one record-only commit. The owner's approval of a decision is still what makes it binding; the commit check does not gate it. This amends the exemption clause of D-42.

## Authority

The owner, 2026-10-22, under W-110.
