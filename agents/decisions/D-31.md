# D-31. Agents commit task-scoped work


Decided 2026-10-19 by the owner, from the workflow review (`agents/workflow-review/agent-comms.md`, messages 9 to 13). Agents may create task-scoped commits, including intermediate commits, without being asked each time. No second item becomes active until the first item's tracked changes are committed. An item may close on the verification it requires. `make docker-builder docker-test` is reserved for integration checkpoints. `AGENTS.md` carries the authorization. It does not cover pushing.

The reason: finished items (B-43 to B-46) sat uncommitted for hours in a working tree shared by several tasks. B-45 and B-46 were overwritten and lost, and `B-43 through B-46/B-18` had to commit six items together.
