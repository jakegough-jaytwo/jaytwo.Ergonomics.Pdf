# D-36. Legacy v1 state leaves the tree

## Context

D-33 archived the four pre-migration state files byte-for-byte in `agents/archive/state-v1/` and kept closed B items there as canonical history. Every open B item is now a W item, every legacy decision is a `D-NN` file, and reusable facts moved to `agents/evidence/` and `agents/reference/`. No tool or skill reads the archive.

## Decision

Remove `agents/archive/state-v1/`. W-56 records its removal; its pre-removal tree state is [squashed]. Closed B items are materialized as W records under D-37 and W-60. Reopening one follows D-33 and D-37: reopen its W record and retain the B ID in `legacy_ids`.

This supersedes D-33's rule that closed B items stay in the tree's archive, and D-35's statement that `archive/state-v1/` stays. The rest of D-33 stands.

## Consequences

Reusable evidence and reference files that cite the archive as their migration source identify D-36 and W-56 as the removal record; the archive's pre-removal tree state is [squashed]. Paths to the archive in decisions, reports, and closed work items remain as historical facts.

The owner, 2026-10-19.
