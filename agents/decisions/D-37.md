# D-37. Closed B items become closed W records

## Context

D-33 turned the open B items into W records and left the closed ones in the archive. D-36 and W-56 removed that archive, and D-35 and W-55 removed the reports it cited; those pre-removal tree states are [squashed]. The work-item directory showed six of the 47 legacy items.

## Decision

Each closed B item has a W record with the same number, status `done` or `cancelled`, and the B ID in `legacy_ids`. The record follows `agents/templates/work-item.md`. Its Problem, scope, and acceptance come from the item's earliest full backlog entry, or from the report that filed it when the backlog only ever held the closed form. Its Closeout comes from the closed entry and the reports it cites. `allowed_changes` lists the paths the item's reports say it changed, including paths that no longer exist. Cross-references to other items use W IDs. Report paths, finding IDs such as QA-B36-01, and quoted text keep their B IDs. History that was never committed is recorded as missing, not reconstructed.

This supersedes D-36's statement that closed B items exist only in the removed archive. The rest of D-36 stands. The backlog history, whose pre-removal tree state is [squashed], remains the source, and these records are frozen like any other closed work item.

## Consequences

`agents/work-items/` holds every legacy item. `make agents-status` and the validator see them. Reopening a closed B item now reopens its existing W record instead of creating one. Report paths and archive citations in other records are not rewritten.

The owner, 2026-10-19, under W-60.
