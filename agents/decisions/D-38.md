# D-38. Two-phase charter: read and append, then new files

## Context

The owner's charter for the library is six business operations: read, fill, and flatten AcroForms; read and write metadata; read encrypted files and their restrictions; PKCS#11 signing; extract pages into a new PDF; and concatenate PDFs. From the first commit, `README.md` and `AGENTS.md` instead listed merge, split, rotate, and creating a PDF as out of scope, and did not mention flattening. Work since then has qualified that narrower scope. The owner reviewed the gap on 2026-10-19 and set a new scope through an interview.

## Decision

The product ships in two phases, divided by the byte-prefix rule: Phase 1 reads and appends incremental updates; Phase 2 writes new files.

- **Phase 1** is release 1.0 and ships production-qualified on its own. Its specification is `docs/spec/phase-1.md`. It adds to today's scope: form-data export, bulk all-or-nothing fill, flatten, rotate, signature inspection, revision listing, attachment relationship, dates, checksum, optional compression, and open-to-attachments; ECDSA P-256 and P-384, SHA-384 and SHA-512, PAdES baseline B-B; async signer and save; hybrid XFA removal on fill; usage-rights handling; strict reading with a documented list of tolerated writer quirks.
- **Phase 2** ships in 1.x: page extraction, concatenation, compaction, removing revisions, and removing passwords. Its specification is `docs/spec/phase-2.md`, a draft until a Phase 2 interview settles its behavior.
- Deferred to 1.x: caller-supplied fonts for non-WinAnsi appearances, and PAdES long-term validation.
- The public API may change freely until 1.0.
- Targets become `netstandard2.0`, `net8.0`, and `net10.0`.
- `QUALIFICATION.md` is right-sized as `docs/spec/phase-1.md` section 10 describes.
- The agent workflow is to be slimmed.

## Consequences

`docs/spec/phase-1.md` and `docs/spec/phase-2.md` disagree with the current charter in `AGENTS.md` and `README.md`, the target frameworks in D-13, and the gates in `QUALIFICATION.md`. Until follow-up items update those files with the owner's approval, implementation of new scope must not start. Release-qualification work for the old scope (W-13, W-14, W-18) should be re-planned against the new specs instead of continuing as written.

This supersedes D-13 for target frameworks once the follow-up item changes them.

## Authority

The owner, 2026-10-19, in the scoping interview recorded under W-61.
