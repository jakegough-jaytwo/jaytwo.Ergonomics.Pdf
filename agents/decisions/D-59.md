# D-59. Defect fixtures are separate from the G2 corpus

## Context

Since W-13, every committed fixture PDF has had a G2 corpus manifest entry. W-99 added five pypdf-generated fixtures for the unsigned `/P` writer quirk (D-57), one per standard security revision, without entries, and the R1 checkpoint failed (W-134). As corpus entries they would take the corpus to 29 externally authored files of 46, below the `QUALIFICATION.md` section 5 rule that two thirds come from tools other than the repository's test generators. pyHanko 0.38.0 also cannot open the revision 5 file. Requiring every regression fixture to join G2 would discourage committed regression fixtures.

## Decision

1. The G2 corpus is `test/jaytwo.Pdf.Tests/Fixtures/*.pdf` and `Fixtures/corpus/*.pdf`. Each has a manifest entry, and the corpus rules apply to it alone.
2. A fixture made for one defect or writer quirk goes in `Fixtures/defects/` and is not a corpus entry. `Fixtures/defects/fixtures.json` records each one: its hash, work item, producer, generating command, what it exercises, the tests that use it, and its validators with the property each establishes.
3. A validator result is `pass`, `fail`, `not-run`, or `unsupported`. An `unsupported` result names the tool version and the reason, and it is never counted as a pass. Another tool that checks the property is recorded separately.
4. A fixture PDF anywhere else under `Fixtures/` fails `CorpusManifestTests`. That test and `DefectFixtureTests` list files and open none, so they run in the fast lane, where a fixture is added.

## Consequences

- W-140 moves the five W-99 fixtures into `Fixtures/defects/`, with pyHanko `unsupported` on revision 5.
- The spec section 8 fixture for the unsigned `/P` quirk is the pypdf-produced fixture D-57 accepted. A defect fixture made by a generator records the producer version that made it.
- The G2 corpus is unchanged: 41 entries, 29 externally authored.

The owner, 2026-10-24, approving option A after an outside review of the W-140 brief.
