## Font-limit reconciliation

**Decided:** A4 (d′) selects resource-limit refusal for a complete supplied font program of **33,554,432 bytes or more**. It keeps call restoration, earlier edits, proportional processing/retention and controlled hostile-structure refusal. [Independent research](research/w218-font-limit-evidence.md), with [raw records](research/w218-font-limit-evidence.json), found LXGW WenKai at 25,486,932 bytes (24.31 MiB) as the largest eligible font. The 8/16 MiB candidates would refuse fonts established embedders accept (A4 trigger); 32 MiB refuses none found. No target-library measurement ran. Beyond LXGW WenKai (24.31 MiB), there is no measured eligible real font through 32 MiB or above; finding one reopens compatibility review and the A4 route if representative and accepted by established embedders. This records only the numeric decision and its bounded evidence, not further A1 progress.

**Earlier review question, superseded by the engineering disposition above:** retain the selected font boundary and propose that XMP also refuse at/above 33,554,432 bytes in a later approved contract/implementation, or keep XMP's inclusive maximum as an explicitly different operation rule?
