## Owner direction for recommendations (2026-10-31)

**Be practical about items orthogonal to the charter.** The charter asks for common operations on real-world PDFs, done dependably and at quality at least equal to established tools; it does not ask for precision or machinery users cannot observe. Recommend in terms of what a user can see or rely on (no clipped text, clear accept/refuse, determinism, predictable resources, compatibility), and leave internal method to engineering. Where established tools converge, match them; where they diverge, promise the guarantee and leave the number as documented behavior. If a proposal adds cost without a user-visible benefit, say so and offer the simpler promise. Decisions 3, 4 and 5 are the model.

**P-CHOICE-INH (A4 route; proposal, not selected; bring at the adoption review).**

* *Trigger:* an explicit clear of a single-selection choice field has no edition-valid local encoding when its effective V is inherited from a non-terminal ancestor. Local null and `I []` do not clear, an empty string is a value, and `V []` is outside the single-selection typing though PDFium and MuPDF accept it.
* *Exact candidate wording (recommended):* "An explicit clear of a single-selection choice field whose effective value is inherited from a non-terminal ancestor refuses with an unsupported-structure result that names the ancestor, and changes nothing. A MultiSelect field clears by writing local `V []` and `I []`; any other choice field clears by removing its local V and I."
* *Alternatives:* (a) write `V []` for the single-selection case (reader-tolerated, non-conformant by the typing); (b) change the ancestor or restructure (excluded by owner direction); (c) the recommended refusal.
* *Evidence:* [VALUE-C5 record](research/w218-form-semantics.md#independent-reader-experiments-choice-storage-clearing-and-language-escapes); corpus: 0 of 813 terminal fields and 0 of 7 choice fields inherit V from such an ancestor (13 unencrypted AcroForm files, no list boxes, small sample). Acceptance impact in the sample is nil but is not representative.
* *Recommendation:* (c), because (a) knowingly writes a value the format does not define for that type.
* This is the only decision identified. It does not block drafting; it returns at the adoption review with the identified revision.

Also stated in forms as a low-impact choice that returns under A4 only if the owner disagrees: new additive writes of text containing U+001B are refused as unrepresentable (no corpus text string contains ESC).
